\section*{The master theorem}

\noindent
The volume's functorial theorem is the ambient-qualified
assignment
\[
 \boxed{\;
 \Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}\colon
 \mathrm{ChirAlg}^{\omega,\mathrm{BL}}_{X}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathrm{HT\text{-}QFT}_{X \times \R},
 \qquad
 \cA \longmapsto T_\cA,
 \;}
\]
Universal Holography
(Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-master},
Chapter~\ref{ch:programme-climax-platonic}). The source category
consists of chirally Koszul algebras with conformal vector at
non-critical level (notation $\omega$), a chosen boundary
linearisation (notation $\mathrm{BL}$), and the exact HT
prefactorisation data required by the comparison theorem. The
target is 3d holomorphic-topological gauge theories on
$X \times \R$. Under these hypotheses the functor sends $\cA$ to
$T_\cA$ with
\[
 \mathrm{Obs}^\partial(T_\cA) \simeq \cA,
 \qquad
 \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{bulk}}(T_\cA) \simeq
 \cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA),
\]
the second equivalence as $E_3$-topological factorisation
algebras in the ambient where the boundary algebra is closed:
on the original chain complex for finite-propagation strata, and on
the weight-completed / KS-$\rho$ Banach ambient for class~M unless
finite propagation is separately verified. This ambient qualifier is
part of the theorem: it is the mechanism that prevents the
holomorphic-topological bulk theorem from claiming raw direct-sum
chain-level strictness in the class~M regime before convergence has
been proved.

The non-logarithmic standard landscape is class-dependent. Classes
$\mathbf G$, $\mathbf L$, and $\mathbf C$ have ordinary-chain
realisations on their stated non-critical loci. Class~$\mathbf M$
is proved in the weight-completed / pro ambient, with raw
direct-sum descent governed by finite propagation. Schellekens
$c=24$ rows and Monster $V^\natural$ require finite-orbifold BV
descent and Dijkgraaf--Witten anomaly trivialisation in their
strata. Irrational cosets enter through the VSKR+BGG tempered
criterion.
Logarithmic $W(p)$ tempering requires the regular
Adamovi\'c--Milas $TW/WW$ character-amplitude bound and the logarithmic
boundary estimate of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}: Gurarie 1993
and Flohr 1996 exhibit logarithmic-CFT amplitudes with unbounded
Massey products despite finite-dimensional Zhu, so the
Zhu-bounded-Massey route does not close $W(p)$.

\section*{The universal stage chain (reading discipline)}

\noindent
Every working object in the volume occupies one of five stages of
the universal chain
\[
\boxed{\;
\underbrace{\mathsf{P}}_{\substack{\text{primitive}}}
 \xrightarrow{\;\alpha\;}
\underbrace{\mathsf{C}}_{\substack{\text{chart}}}
 \xrightarrow{\;\beta\;}
\underbrace{\mathsf{S}}_{\substack{\text{shadow}}}
 \xrightarrow{\;\gamma\;}
\underbrace{\mathsf{Z}}_{\substack{\text{centre} \\ = \text{bulk}}}
 \xrightarrow{\;\delta\;}
\underbrace{\mathsf{A}}_{\substack{\text{acting} \\ \text{object}}}
\;}
\]
with arrows $\alpha$ \textup(chart-projection\textup), $\beta$
\textup(comparison / functor / natural transformation\textup),
$\gamma$ \textup(ambient declaration\textup), $\delta$
\textup(endpoint hypothesis / convergence\textup), and an
ambient effectiveness type $\epsilon$
\textup(orientation / non-degeneracy\textup) sitting outside the
chain but conditioning every other arrow. Cross-stage collapse ---
identifying objects at different stages without naming the
licensing arrow --- is the master pattern \emph{shadow $=$ object}
that the licensing discipline forbids.

The volume's primitive object lives at level $\mathsf{P}$: the
bicoloured primitive package
$\primPkgBicolour$
on the curve--line pair $(C, \R)$
\textup(\cref{def:bicoloured-primitive-package},
\cref{thm:bicoloured-primitive-universality}\textup).
Choosing a boundary vacuum $b$ projects $\mathsf{P} \to \mathsf{C}$
to the chart algebra $\bdyAlg = A_b = \mathrm{End}_{\openFactCat}(b)$
\textup(licensing $\alpha$\textup). The chiral bar coalgebra
$\BarTwc{A_b}$ at level $\mathsf{S}$ is the twisting-coupling
shadow of $A_b$ \textup(licensing $\beta$\textup); it is
\emph{not} the bulk. The level-$\mathsf{Z}$ object is the algebraic
derived chiral centre
$\Zderch(A_b) \simeq \bulkChirHoch{A_b} = \mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet(A_b, A_b)$
\textup(\cref{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}, the Hochschild concentration theorem\textup);
its physical bulk reading requires the scoped HT open/closed
comparison.
Line operators on $A_b$ live at level $\mathsf{A}$, the acting
object on the centre. Each step from $\mathsf{P}$ to $\mathsf{A}$
requires a named licensing datum; this is the reading discipline
of the volume.

The seven heptagon faces of $\SCchtop$
\textup(\cref{rem:heptagon-licensing-duality}\textup) and the
five licensing types $\alpha / \beta / \gamma / \delta / \epsilon$
are mutually dual: face $=$ stage-pair to be licensed; type $=$
data needed to license the stage-pair. Throughout the volume,
every theorem statement carries the relevant licensing tags
inline.

The cyclic-Hochschild four-object discipline
\textup(\cref{thm:cyclic-hochschild-stage-stratification}\textup)
places $\mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet$, $\mathrm{ChirHoch}_\bullet$,
$\mathrm{HC}^{\mathrm{cyc}}$, $\mathrm{HC}^-$ at distinct levels
of the chain with their respective $\mathsf{S} \to \mathsf{Z}$ or
$\mathsf{S} \to \mathsf{A}$ promotions; only
$\mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet$ is the bulk, the others are shadows
requiring further licensing. The $\kappa$-tuple
$\kappaTuple{\cA}$ is the level-$\mathsf{S}$ characteristic
\textup(no scalar collapse;
\cref{thm:kappa-tuple-primitivity-orthogonal-shimura}\textup):
the per-row constructions are independent functions on the
period domain. Universal Holography $\Phihol$ is the universal
stage chain made physical
\textup(\cref{cor:universal-holography-via-bicoloured-universality}\textup):
the $3$d HT QFT image of an $\SCchtop$-algebra is the universal
stage chain action on its bicoloured primitive package presentation.

\section*{From algebraic geometry to physics}

algebra is the local-operator algebra of the corresponding 3d

The shadow tower of $\Theta_{\mathrm{Vir}_c}$ classifies the
nonlinear complexity of the gravitational dynamics; its five

\section*{The coproduct: topological factorisation along $\R$}

The same bar complex carries a coassociative coproduct from
deconcatenation:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-delta}
\Delta[s^{-1}a_1 \,|\, \cdots \,|\, s^{-1}a_n]
\;=\;
\sum_{i=0}^{n}
[s^{-1}a_1 \,|\, \cdots \,|\, s^{-1}a_i]
\;\otimes\;
[s^{-1}a_{i+1} \,|\, \cdots \,|\, s^{-1}a_n].
\end{equation}
Place $n$ fields at ordered positions $t_1 < \cdots < t_n$ on
$\R$ and choose a cut $t_i < t_* < t_{i+1}$; the value of
$\Delta$ at $i$ records the factorisation. The ordered
configuration space $\Conf_n^<(\R) = \{t_1 < \cdots < t_n\}$ is
contractible (open simplex), so the $\Eone$-coalgebra structure
contracts to strict deconcatenation; the topological coproduct alone
introduces no higher homotopies; the
coproduct is the unique coassociative coproduct on
$T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA)$, determined by cogenerators.

$t_1 < t_2$. Apply $\Delta$:
\[
\Delta[s^{-1}J | s^{-1}J]
\;=\;
[\,] \otimes [s^{-1}J | s^{-1}J]
\;+\;
[s^{-1}J] \otimes [s^{-1}J]
\;+\;
[s^{-1}J | s^{-1}J] \otimes [\,].
\]
Three terms = three cuts. Middle term is factorisation into
independent half-line observables (possible because $J_{(0)}J = 0$:
no simple-pole interaction). Apply $d_{\barB}$ to middle:
$d_{\barB}([s^{-1}J]) = 0$ (no unary OPE), commuting with the
cut. Coderivation property
$\Delta \circ d_{\barB} = (d_{\barB} \otimes \id +
\id \otimes d_{\barB}) \circ \Delta$ states: collisions in

\section*{Construction of the Swiss-cheese operad $\SCchtop$}

The structures above (differential, coproduct, $\Ainf$
operations, $R$-matrix, Koszul triangle) are governed by a
single two-coloured operad.

\paragraph{Colours and spaces of operations.}
Colours $\{\mathsf{ch}, \mathsf{top}\}$: closed (holomorphic)
and open (topological).

\emph{Closed-to-closed.}
$\SCchtop(\mathsf{ch}^k;\,\mathsf{ch}) = \FM_k(\C)$, the
Fulton--MacPherson compactification of $k$ labelled points in
$\C$. Smooth manifold with corners of real dimension
$2k - 3$ (for $k \ge 2$, after quotienting by translations and
dilations); codimension-1 strata $D_S$ ($|S| \ge 2$): collision
divisor with internal cluster configuration in $\FM_{|S|}(\C)$
and cluster position in $\FM_{k - |S| + 1}(\C)$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-FM-fibration}
\FM_{|S|}(\C) \;\hookrightarrow\; D_S \;\twoheadrightarrow\;
\FM_{k - |S| + 1}(\C).
\end{equation}
Convention: $\FM_0(\C) = \FM_1(\C) = \mathrm{pt}$.

\emph{Mixed-to-open.}
$\SCchtop(\mathsf{ch}^k, \mathsf{top}^m;\,\mathsf{top}) =
\FM_{k,m}(\mathbb H)$, the Fulton--MacPherson--Voronov
compactification of $k$ interior points of the closed upper
half-plane $\mathbb H$ and $m$ boundary points on
$\partial\mathbb H = \R$, modulo boundary-preserving affine maps.
Its depth-zero associated graded is the decoupled product
$\FM_k(\C) \times \Eone(m)$ with the little-intervals operad
$\Eone(m) = \Conf_m^{<}(\R)$: what locality forces is this
splitting of the associated graded --- a local observable
factorising holomorphically in $z$ and locally constant in $t$
depends, away from the boundary-incidence strata, only on
collision data in $\C$ and time-ordering in $\R$ --- while the
incidence strata of $\FM_{k,m}(\mathbb H)$ record the
bulk-to-boundary coupling that the product forgets.

\emph{Open-to-closed (empty).}
$\SCchtop(\ldots, \mathsf{top}, \ldots;\,\mathsf{ch}) = \varnothing$.
Operadic expression of the physical principle: bulk
interactions restrict to boundaries, not conversely.

\paragraph{Composition.}
By substitution of compactified configurations.

\emph{Closed-into-closed.}
$\gamma_{\mathrm{ch}}\colon \FM_k(\C) \times \prod_i
\FM_{\ell_i}(\C) \to \FM_{\sum_i \ell_i}(\C)$ via FM blow-up
(Fulton--MacPherson~\cite{FM94}, Getzler--Jones~\cite{GJ94}).

\emph{Closed-into-open.}
Closed operations are substituted at interior points of
$\FM_{k,m}(\mathbb H)$; no open operation may be substituted into a
closed input slot. Forced at three levels: geometrically (no
configuration space interpolates real-line data to holomorphic
collision data); D-module level (!-extension support vanishing
from codim-$1$ boundary); physically (the factorized singular
parametrix has separate holomorphic and topological factors, so
topological inputs do not contribute to holomorphic outputs).

\emph{Mixed-into-open.}
Mixed operations are substituted at boundary points, the boundary
point carrying the transverse position of the inserted
configuration; at depth zero this reduces to the componentwise
product of $\gamma_{\mathrm{ch}}$ with the standard $\Eone$
composition.

\begin{center}
\emph{Composition is associative, equivariant, unital by
inheritance from the Fulton--MacPherson insertion of compactified
configurations
(Definition~\textup{\ref{def:SC-composition}}).}
\end{center}

\begin{observation}[Helicity as Swiss-cheese directionality]
\label{obs:helicity-directionality}
The two $\SCchtop$ colours are, in the celestial reading, the
two helicities of scattering amplitudes. Positive-helicity
insertions are holomorphic (closed colour, on $\FM_k(\C)$,
producing $\omega_k \in \Omega^\bullet_{\log}(\ov{\FM}_k(\C))$
generating the bar differential). Negative-helicity insertions
are topological (open colour, on $\Conf_m^{<}(\R)$, producing
the $\Eone$ coproduct). The no-open-to-closed rule models the
helicity selection rule: negative-helicity gluons cannot produce
positive-helicity gluons by OPE alone. The MHV amplitude is the
minimal open--closed coupling under $\SCchtop$ once the comparison
data below are supplied; all-plus and single-minus amplitudes vanish
for the same form-type obstruction reason on that comparison surface.
Self-dual sector identification is proved via
$\barBch(\cA)$ in
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:ch-core-helicity-splitting}(i)}
(Part~\textup{VII}).  The anti-self-dual, mixed-helicity, and MHV
clauses of Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:ch-core-helicity-splitting}} are
conditional on the Verdier-helicity comparison and the tree-level
Mellin/Parke--Taylor propagator comparison stated there.  The remaining
datum is the form-type comparison for the mixed-helicity twisting
morphism; it is not an unconditional consequence of \(\SCchtop\) alone.
\end{observation}

\section*{The $\Ainf$ chiral operations $m_k$}

Let $\bar a:=|a|-1$ denote the suspended degree. The reduced chiral
bar coalgebra is
\[
\barBch(\cA)=T^c(s^{-1}\overline{\cA})
=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}(s^{-1}\overline{\cA})^{\otimes n}.
\]
An $\Ainf$ chiral structure is a degree-$1$ coderivation $D_\cA$ of
$\barBch(\cA)$. Its cogenerator projections are the chiral operations
\[
m_k \colon A^{\otimes k} \longrightarrow
A((\lambda_1))\cdots((\lambda_{k-1})),
\qquad k \geq 1,\quad \deg m_k=2-k,
\]
with spectral parameters $\lambda_i=z_i-z_k$. Explicitly, for
homogeneous inputs,
\begin{multline}
\label{eq:intro-ainfty-coderivation}
D_\cA\bigl[s^{-1}a_1|\cdots|s^{-1}a_n\bigr]  \\
=
\sum_{\substack{k\ge1\\1\le i\le n-k+1}}
(-1)^{\epsilon(i,k)}
\bigl[s^{-1}a_1|\cdots|
s^{-1}m_k(a_i,\ldots,a_{i+k-1})|\cdots|s^{-1}a_n\bigr],
\qquad
\epsilon(i,k)=\sum_{r<i}\bar a_r .
\end{multline}
The deconcatenation coproduct satisfies
\[
\Delta D_\cA=(D_\cA\otimes\id+\id\otimes D_\cA)\Delta.
\]
The whole $\Ainf$ chiral equation is $D_\cA^2=0$; the displayed
Stasheff relation below is the arity-$n$ cogenerator projection after
translating \eqref{eq:intro-ainfty-coderivation} into the desuspended
coordinate convention fixed in Definition~\ref{def:ainfty-relations}.

\paragraph{The $\Ainf$ relations as boundary decompositions of
$\FM_n(\C)$.}
\begin{multline}
\label{eq:intro-ainfty}
\sum_{\substack{i+j = n+1 \\ i,j \geq 1}} \;\sum_{s=0}^{n-j}
(-1)^{|a_1| + \cdots + |a_s|}\;
m_i\bigl(a_1, \ldots, a_s,\,
m_j(a_{s+1}, \ldots, a_{s+j};\, \lambda_{s+1}, \ldots,
\lambda_{s+j-1}) \;\big|\;\\
\Lambda_{[s+1,\ldots,s+j]},\,
a_{s+j+1}, \ldots, a_n;\, \bm\lambda^{\mathrm{out}}\bigr) = 0,
\end{multline}
$\Lambda_{[s+1,\ldots,s+j]} = \lambda_{s+1} + \cdots +
\lambda_{s+j-1}$ effective spectral parameter of fused block.
The relation is Stokes theorem on $\FM_n(\C)$, term-by-term:
\[
\begin{array}{l|l}
\text{Algebraic datum} & \text{Geometric meaning} \\
\hline
\text{divisor } D_S,\ |S|\ge 2 &
 \text{cluster of } |S| \text{ points colliding} \\
\text{inner } m_{|S|} & \text{residue along } D_S \\
\text{outer } m_{n-|S|+1} &
 \text{fused cluster interacting with remainder} \\
(-1)^{|a_1|+\cdots+|a_s|} &
 \text{Koszul sign (coderivation past first } s\text{)} \\
\lambda_{\mathrm{inner}} \mapsto
 \Lambda_{[s+1,\ldots,s+j]} &
 \text{momentum conservation (fused cluster)}
\end{array}
\]

For $n = 2$: $m_1 \circ m_2 + m_2 \circ
(m_1 \otimes \id + \id \otimes m_1) = 0$, $Q = m_1$ a graded
derivation of $m_2$.

For $n = 3$: codim-1 strata of $\FM_3(\C)$ are $D_{\{1,2\}}$,
$D_{\{2,3\}}$, $D_{\{1,3\}}$. In ordered $A_\infty$ convention,
only consecutive blocks contribute to the bar coderivation:
$D_{\{1,2\}}$ and $D_{\{2,3\}}$ give the two
$m_2(m_2(-,-),-)$ terms; $D_{\{1,3\}}$ (non-consecutive) does
not contribute at the bar-complex level, but contributes to the
PVA Jacobi identity after passing to the unordered shadow
(see the final Arnold-boundary identity in
Theorem~\ref{thm:stokes_arnold}). The relation
reads:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-n3}
m_2(m_2(a,b;\lambda_1), c;\, \lambda_1 + \lambda_2) \;-\;
m_2(a, m_2(b,c;\lambda_2);\, \lambda_1) \;+\;
m_1 m_3(a,b,c) \;+\; m_3(Q\cdot) \;=\; 0.
\end{equation}
On cohomology, $m_3$ terms vanish and the surviving identity is
the Jacobi identity for the $\lambda$-bracket. At chain level,
$m_3$ is the homotopy for failure of strict associativity.

\paragraph{Resolvent principle: one equation for all $m_k$.}
$\{m_k\}_{k \ge 2}$ are not independent. With $(H, p, \iota, h)$
a deformation retract of $(\cA, m_1)$ onto cohomology with
$h^2 = h\iota = ph = 0$, the dressed propagator
$\Phi\colon T^+(H) \to \cA$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-resolvent}
\Phi \;=\; \iota \;+\; h \circ m_2 \circ \Phi^{\otimes 2}
\end{equation}
the tree-level bootstrap (Theorem~\ref{thm:resolvent-principle}).
Solution by iteration: $m_k^H$ as a sum over $C_{k-1}$ planar
binary trees (equivalently, binary trees with $n = k - 1$
internal vertices and $k$ leaves), with $m_2$ at vertices and
$h$ at edges. One binary input, one homotopy, all higher
operations.

\paragraph{Regular/singular decomposition of $m_2$.}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-reg-sing}
m_2(a,b)(\lambda) \;=\;
\underbrace{m_2^{\mathrm{reg}}(a,b)}_{\in\, A[[\lambda]]}
\;+\;
\underbrace{m_2^{\mathrm{sing}}(a,b)}_{\in\, \lambda^{-1}
A[\lambda^{-1}]}.
\end{equation}
Regular: normally ordered product (commutative multiplication).
Singular: $\lambda$-bracket (OPE residue):
$a \cdot b := m_2^{\mathrm{reg}}(a,b)|_{\lambda=0}$,
$\{a \,{}_\lambda\, b\} := m_2^{\mathrm{sing}}(a,b)$.
Together they form a Poisson vertex algebra on
$H^\bullet(A, Q)$.


\section*{PVA descent: from $\Ainf$ to Poisson vertex algebra}

\paragraph{The product.}
On $Q$-closed $a, b$, the regular part $a \cdot b$ is
well-defined commutative product (Vacuum: if $a = Qc$, $m_2(Qc,
b) = Q m_2(c,b) \pm m_2(c, Qb)$; regular at $\lambda = 0$ is
$Q$-exact). Commutativity from $\sigma\colon \FM_2(\C) \to
\FM_2(\C)$ exchanging the two points: $\lambda \to -\lambda - \partial$.

\paragraph{The $\lambda$-bracket.}
Singular part satisfies sesquilinearity (chain-level
sesquilinearity of $m_2$, geometric translation covariance on
$\FM_2(\C)$): $\{\partial a {}_\lambda b\} = -\lambda \{a {}_\lambda b\}$,
$\{a {}_\lambda \partial b\} = (\partial + \lambda)\{a {}_\lambda b\}$.
Skew-symmetry: $\sigma$ on $\FM_2(\C)$ sends
$\lambda \to -\lambda - \partial$;
$\{a {}_\lambda b\} = -\{b {}_{-\lambda - \partial} a\}$.
Leibniz: $n = 2$ $\Ainf$ relation with product yields
$\{c {}_\lambda (a \cdot b)\} = \{c {}_\lambda a\} \cdot b +
a \cdot \{c {}_\lambda b\}$.

\paragraph{Higher operations and the PVA shadow.}
The PVA descent does not require the unconditional vanishing of
$m_k$ for $k\ge3$. The higher operations are chain-level homotopies
and obstruction classes; they disappear from the binary PVA shadow,
but they vanish on cohomology only on a formal or compatibly
nullhomotopic locus. In Section~\ref{sec:PVA_descent} this extra
input is named as a compatible topological bounding-chain datum.

\paragraph{Jacobi identity.}
Jacobi requires $n = 3$ $\Ainf$ relation and Stokes on
$\FM_3(\C)$. Apply~\eqref{eq:intro-n3} on $Q$-closed $a, b, c$;
the $m_3$ contribution appears as a $Q$-boundary, and $m_1 = Q$
vanishes on the inputs. Surviving:
$m_2(m_2(a,b;\lambda_1), c;\, \lambda_1 + \lambda_2) =
m_2(a, m_2(b,c;\lambda_2);\, \lambda_1)$. Singular parts give
LHS and RHS of Jacobi identity; mixed terms cancel by Arnold
relation $\omega_{12}\wedge\omega_{23} + \omega_{23}\wedge\omega_{31}
+ \omega_{31}\wedge\omega_{12} = 0$ on $\Conf_3(\C)$ via Stokes
on boundary strata of $\FM_3(\C)$. Full verification:
Theorem~\ref{thm:cohomology_PVA}, Section~\ref{sec:PVA_descent}.

\begin{maintheorem}[Cohomological descent to PVA;
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:cohomology_PVA}}]
\label{thm:intro-PVA-descent}
Let $\cA$ be a logarithmic $\SCchtop$-algebra
\textup{(}Definition~\textup{\ref{def:log-SC-algebra}}: an
$\SCchtop$-algebra whose structure maps extend to logarithmic
differential forms on the FM compactification, i.e.\ propagator
is a log $1$-form with at worst simple poles along boundary
divisors\textup{)}. Then $H^\bullet(\cA, Q)$ carries the
structure of a $(-1)$-shifted Poisson vertex algebra
($\lambda$-bracket has cohomological degree $-1$; underlying Lie
bracket lives on $A[1]$):
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item commutative product is the regular part of $m_2$;
\item $\lambda$-bracket is the singular part of $m_2$;
\end{enumerate}
PVA axioms (sesquilinearity, skew-symmetry, Jacobi, Leibniz,
vacuum) are proved by Stokes on $\FM_n(\C)$. Higher-operation
vanishing is equivalent to the compatible topological
bounding-chain datum of Proposition~\ref{prop:m3_vanish}: the
classes $[m_k]\in H^\bullet(\operatorname{End}_{\SCchtop}(\cA))$
are killed by explicit homotopies $h_k$.
\end{maintheorem}

The PVA is the classical shadow: it remembers the commutative
product and singular OPE, but forgets the chain-level homotopies
$m_3, m_4, \ldots$.

\section*{The spectral $R$-matrix from the $\lambda$-bracket}

The $\lambda$-bracket encodes the classical braiding of line
operators. Bridge from PVA to Yang--Baxter: Laplace transform.

\paragraph{The classical $r$-matrix.}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-laplace}
r(z) \;=\; \int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda z}\,
\{{\cdot}\,{}_\lambda\,{\cdot}\}\, d\lambda.
\end{equation}
Convergent for $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0$ under the
physical-realization hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{thm:physics-bridge}; meromorphic on $\C^\times$.
Algebraically: if $\{a {}_\lambda b\} = \sum_{n \ge 0} c_n \lambda^n / n!$,
then $r(z)(a \otimes b) = \sum_{n \ge 0} c_n / z^{n+1}$.

\emph{PVA Jacobi and CYBE.} The Laplace transform sends the PVA
Jacobi identity to a spectral kernel identity. For a non-dynamical
constant collision tensor this identity is the classical Yang--Baxter
equation:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-CYBE}
[r_{12}(z_{12}),\, r_{13}(z_{13})] +
[r_{12}(z_{12}),\, r_{23}(z_{23})] +
[r_{13}(z_{13}),\, r_{23}(z_{23})] \;=\; 0.
\end{equation}
$\mu = \lambda_2$, $\lambda = \lambda_1$ becomes
$z_{13} = z_{12} + z_{23}$ under Laplace. For field-dependent PVA
kernels the same transform is the PVA Jacobiator in spectral
coordinates, not an ordinary non-dynamical CYBE.

\paragraph{The quantum $R$-matrix.}
$R(z)$ from bulk-to-boundary OPE of line operators: parallel
boundary lines $L_1(z_1), L_2(z_2)$ in 3d HT theory on
$\C \times \R_{\ge 0}$ with braiding
\[
R_{L_1, L_2}(z) \colon L_1 \otimes L_2
\;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\; L_2 \otimes L_1,
\qquad z = z_1 - z_2,
\]
meromorphic, computed by configuration integrals over
$\FM_2(\C) \times \Eone(2)$
(Definition~\ref{def:spectral-braiding}). Semiclassical limit
$R(z) = \id + \h\, r(z) + O(\h^2)$ recovers $r(z)$.

\emph{Yang--Baxter.}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-YBE}
R_{12}(z_{12})\, R_{13}(z_{13})\, R_{23}(z_{23})
\;=\;
R_{23}(z_{23})\, R_{13}(z_{13})\, R_{12}(z_{12}),
\end{equation}
proved by Stokes on $\FM_3(\C)$. Three codim-1 strata contribute
the three double products on each side; vanishing of the
boundary integral
$\int_{\partial \FM_3(\C)} \omega_3^{\partial} = 0$ forces
equality. $D_{\{1,3\}}$ does not contribute independently:
spectral parameter $z_{13} = z_{12} + z_{23}$.

\emph{Classical limit.} CYBE is the semiclassical limit of YBE;
both follow from Stokes on $\FM_3(\C)$.

\section*{The bulk--boundary--line Koszul triangle}

A 3d HT theory on $\C \times \R$ produces three algebraic
objects:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \textbf{bulk chiral algebra} $\Abulk$: local operators on
 $\C \times \R$;
\item \textbf{boundary algebra} $\Bbound$: local operators on
 $\C \times \{0\}$;
\item \textbf{line category} $\cC_{\mathrm{line}}$: line
 operators on $\{z_0\} \times \R_t$.
\end{enumerate}
Strongest proved relation, scoped on the chirally Koszul
locus and under compact-generation / derived-centre hypotheses:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:intro-triangle}
\Abulk \;\simeq\;
\Zder(\Bbound) \;\simeq\;
\Zder(\cC_{\mathrm{line}}) \;\simeq\;
\HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}),
\qquad
\cC_{\mathrm{line}} \;\simeq\; \cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}\text{-}\mathbf{mod}.
\end{equation}
Unconditionally: bulk $\simeq$ chiral Hochschild cochains of the
boundary algebra. Primitive datum is the open-sector
factorization category $\cC$
(Definition~\ref{def:oc-factorization-category}); $B_\partial =
\End_\cC(b)$ and $\cO_{\mathrm{bulk}} = Z_{\mathrm{der}}(\cC)$
are derived invariants depending only on the Morita class of
$\cC$.

\paragraph{Construction of the three vertices.}
\emph{Bulk algebra.} $\Abulk$ is the factorisation algebra of
local observables on $\C \times \R$, with differential
$Q_{\mathrm{BRST}}$ from BV master equation. On cohomology
$H^\bullet(\Abulk, Q)$ is the $(-1)$-shifted PVA of
Theorem~\ref{thm:intro-PVA-descent}.

\emph{Boundary algebra.} A boundary condition
$b\colon \C \times \{0\} \hookrightarrow \C \times \R_{\ge 0}$
defines $\Bbound = \End_{\cC_{\mathrm{line}}}(M_b)$, with
boundary differential $Q_{\partial}$ and full $\Ainf$ chiral
structure from restriction of Swiss-cheese operations to the
half-space.

\emph{Line category.} A line $L$ at $z_0$ defines topological
quantum mechanics on $\R_t$ with state space $V_L$. Bulk
operators near the line act on $V_L$ through the open colour of
$\SCchtop$, the mixed operations
$C_\ast(\FM_k(\C) \times \Eone(m)) \otimes \Abulk^{\otimes k}
\otimes V_L^{\otimes m} \to V_L$. On the chirally Koszul locus
the comparison theorem
\textup{(}Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:lines_as_modules}}\textup{)}
models this by $\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}$-modules: equivalence
$\Phi\colon \cC_{\mathrm{line}} \to
\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}\text{-}\mathbf{mod}$.

\paragraph{Derived centre identification.}
$\Abulk \simeq \Zder(\Bbound)$ proved in the exact
boundary-linear sector
(Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary-linear-bulk-boundary}). Derived
centre $\Zder(\Bbound) := \RHom_{\Bbound^e}(\Bbound, \Bbound)$.
Identification uses derived critical locus technology: $\Abulk =
\dCrit(W)$ (derived zero locus of $dW$ in $T^*X$) for
holomorphic potential $W$; derived centre identified with
$T^*[-1]\dCrit(W)$. Morita invariance:
$\Zder(\Bbound) \simeq \Zder(\cC_{\mathrm{line}}) \simeq
\HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}})$.

\paragraph{Homotopy coherence.}
Triangle~\eqref{eq:intro-triangle} is an equivalence of
homotopy-coherent objects
(Remark~\ref{rem:triangle-homotopy-first}); each vertex is
$\Ainf$ or $\Linf$. Premature strictification at any corner
destroys the equivalence.

\paragraph{The $(\infty, 2)$-adjunction on
$\overline{\mathcal A_2}$.}
Chain-level and $(\infty, 1)$-categorical ambients for bar--cobar
are not two parallel statements of the same theorem; they are
two $1$-cells of one $(\infty, 2)$-adjunction on the Siegel
parameter base $\overline{\mathcal A_2}$. The compatibility
data between the two ambients is the $\infty$-groupoid
\[
 \mathrm{Data}(\cA)
 \;\simeq\;
 B\,\mathrm{Aut}^h_{\Linf}\bigl(\mathfrak g^{\infty,1}_\cA\bigr),
\]
with $\mathfrak g^{\infty,1}_\cA$ the
$(\infty,1)$-categorical chiral deformation dg Lie algebra of
$\cA$. Specialised to the K3 Hall boundary object
$\mathbf H_{\Delta_5}$ of the universal trace identity, the
$\infty$-groupoid collapses to
$\widehat{\mathfrak{grt}}_1 / \mathrm{ob}^{\mathrm{GN}}$, the
Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller completion modulo the Gan--Ginzburg
obstruction subalgebra. The $\SCchtop$ coloured coherence on the
chiral derived centre pair is the $2$-cell data of this
adjunction, not an auxiliary rectification. Every
$\SCchtop$-algebra on the Koszul locus, read simultaneously at
chain level and at $(\infty, 1)$-categorical level, is a single
$(\infty, 2)$-functorial datum on $\overline{\mathcal A_2}$.

\paragraph{Chiral Kontsevich formality.}
Kontsevich's admissible-graph $\Linf$-quasi-isomorphism
$T^{\mathrm{poly}} \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathrm{Hoch}^\bullet$
on $\R^d$ (Kontsevich 2003) admits a chiral upgrade:
\[
 T^{\mathrm{poly}}_{\mathrm{ch}}
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;
 \mathrm{ChirHoch}^\bullet,
\]
transported through the Francis--Gaitsgory
factorisation-$\infty$-operadic lane and the Tamarkin operadic
formality lane simultaneously. The admissible graphs carry
$\eta_{ij}$-weights instead of Kontsevich's hyperbolic

\section*{The dg-shifted Yangian}

On the chirally Koszul locus, $\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}$ carries
two layers of structure: $\SCchtop$-algebra structure from
operadic self-duality (the algebra half), bialgebra structure
from the monoidal category of line operators (the coalgebra
half).

\noindent\textsc{Definition}
\textit{(dg-shifted Yangian; Definition~\textup{\ref{def:dg_Yangian}}).}
A \emph{dg-shifted Yangian} $\mathcal{Y}$ is an $\Ainf$ algebra
with:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item translation automorphism
 $\tau_z = \exp(zT)\colon \mathcal{Y} \to \mathcal{Y}[[z]]$;
\item spectral $R$-matrix
 $r(z) \in \mathcal{Y} \widehat{\otimes}\, \mathcal{Y}[[z^{-1}, z]]$;
\item twisted coproduct $\Delta_z\colon \mathcal{Y} \to
 \mathcal{Y} \otimes_{r(z)} \mathcal{Y}[[z^{-1}, z]]$;
\item counit $\varepsilon\colon \mathcal{Y} \to \C$;
\end{enumerate}
	satisfying counit axioms, translation invariance,
	weak co-commutativity $(\tau_w\otimes\tau_w)r(z)=r(z)$,
	the normalized skew relation $r(-z)=-r_{21}(z)$ only when the
	unitary exchange normalization is part of the comparison surface,
	and the quantum $\Ainf$ Yang--Baxter relation
	$r_{23}(z) + (\id \otimes \Delta_z)(r(z+w)) =
	r_{12}(w) + (\Delta_w \otimes \id)(r(z))$.

The Koszul duality
$(\SCchtop)^! = (\mathsf{Lie}, \mathsf{Ass}, \text{shuffle-mixed})$
(\S\ref{subsec:dg-yangian-operadic}) gives
$\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}$ a $(\SCchtop)^!$-algebra structure;
since $\mathsf{Ass}$ is self-dual the open colour preserves its
$E_1$-algebra structure. The monoidal structure on line
operators on the chirally Koszul locus
($\cC_{\mathrm{line}} \simeq \cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}\text{-}\mathbf{mod}$)
provides the coproduct and $R$-matrix (the bialgebra half).
$\cA^!_{\mathrm{line}}$ carries the dg-shifted Yangian package
(Theorem~\ref{thm:yangian-recognition}), and the full two-colour
Koszul duality involution returns $\cA$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:duality-involution}).

In the affine lineage, the ordered/open sector sharpens the
abstract Yangian package to the familiar Drinfeld and RTT
presentations: $T(u) = \sum T_{ij}(u)\, e_{ij}$ satisfies
$R_{12}(u-v)\, T_1(u)\, T_2(v) = T_2(v)\, T_1(u)\, R_{12}(u-v)$;
RTT is the spectral rewriting of the F2 line-operator-braiding face
of the $r(z)$ $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$-torsor developed in

\section*{The recognition theorem and homotopy-Koszulity}

\begin{maintheorem}[Recognition under logarithmic $\SCchtop$ hypotheses;

\label{thm:intro-recognition}

\end{maintheorem}

Proof (Section~\ref{sec:foundations}) reduces to
$\FM_k(\C) \times \Conf_k(\R)$ decomposition + retract

\begin{maintheorem}[Homotopy-Koszulity at the associated-graded
level; Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:homotopy-Koszul}}]
\label{thm:intro-homotopy-Koszul}
The depth-zero associated graded $\gr\,\SCchtop$ (the decoupled

is homotopy-Koszul: the canonical
$\Omega \mathbf{B}(\gr\,\SCchtop) \xrightarrow{\,\sim\,} \gr\,\SCchtop$ is
a quasi-isomorphism of dg operads. The coupled statement for
$\SCchtop$ itself is Conjecture~\ref{conj:coupled-sc-koszul}:
Livernet's non-formality of the Swiss-cheese operad \cite{Liv15}
obstructs the formality route to it.
\end{maintheorem}

Three facts enter. (1) The decoupled homology operad, built
from individually Koszul $\Etwo$ ($\Etwo(k) = H_\ast(\mathrm{Conf}_k(\C))$,
governing Gerstenhaber algebras) and $\Ass = \Eone$ via the
componentwise distributive law, is Koszul (Ginzburg--Kapranov~\cite{GK94});
the coupled homology Swiss-cheese operad is also Koszul
(Hoefel~\cite{Hoe09}, Hoefel--Livernet~\cite{HL12}), but only the
decoupled statement is used. (2)
Kontsevich formality: $\Phi\colon \gr\,\SCchtop \to \mathsf{sc}^{\mathrm{gr}}$
quasi-isomorphism of two-coloured dg operads (identity on open
colour, formality $C_\ast(\FM_k(\C)) \to \Etwo(k)$ on closed
colour); no such map exists out of the coupled $\SCchtop$
\cite{Liv15}. (3) Bar/cobar preserve qi between augmented operads
(Berger--Moerdijk~\cite{BM03}); two-out-of-three gives
$\Omega \mathbf{B}(\gr\,\SCchtop) \simeq \gr\,\SCchtop$.

Bar-cobar adjunction $(\Omega, \mathbf{B})$ on $\SCchtop$-algebras
is a Quillen equivalence
(Theorem~\ref{thm:scchtop-bar-cobar-adjunction}). Filtered version
(Theorem~\ref{thm:filtered-koszul}) extends beyond quadratic.
$\SCchtop$ is not self-dual: the duality functor is an
involution, but the operad and its dual differ in dimensions.
$(\SCchtop)^! = (\mathsf{Lie}, \mathsf{Ass},
\text{shuffle-mixed})$.

\section*{The topologisation theorem}

The $\SCchtop$ output admits a further structure when the
boundary algebra has conformal vector at non-critical level:
topologisation. Sugawara
$T(z) = \frac{1}{2(k+h^\vee)}\sum_a {:}J^a J^a{:}(z)$ renders
$\C$-translations $Q$-exact on \(Q_{\mathrm{CS}}\)-cohomology, makes the closed
chiral colour topological on cohomology, and produces an
$E_3$-topological algebra on
$H^\bullet_{Q_{\mathrm{CS}}}\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)$. Dunn additivity then combines
$\Etwo^{\mathrm{top}} \times \Eone^{\mathrm{top}} =
E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$: a 3d TQFT, independent of complex structure
on $\C$ after passage to \(Q\)-cohomology. The strict raw-chain-level
lift is a separate statement.

\begin{maintheorem}[Topologisation for affine Kac--Moody;
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:E3-topological-km}}]
\label{thm:intro-e3-km}
For $\widehat{\fg}_k$ at non-critical $k \neq -h^\vee$ with
simple $\fg$,
$H^\bullet_{Q_{\mathrm{CS}}}\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\widehat{\fg}_k)$
carries $E_3$-topological structure. The 3d HT theory is
holomorphic Chern--Simons (Costello--Li, by dimensional
reduction from 6d holomorphic gauge theory).
\end{maintheorem}

\begin{maintheorem}[Topologisation via Drinfeld--Sokolov;
Theorems~\textup{\ref{thm:E3-topological-DS}}
and~\textup{\ref{thm:E3-topological-DS-general}}]
\label{thm:intro-e3-ds}
For every DS reduction covered by the Costello--Gaiotto boundary
condition, with a good DS grading and non-critical level
(principal and the stated hook/subregular non-principal families),
the chiral derived centre carries $E_3$-topological structure after
passing to the cohomology of the total DS differential
\(Q_{\mathrm{DS}}\), conditional on the DS BRST primitive package

remaining input is the normalised cohomological identity
\[
T_{\mathrm{DS}}=[Q_{\mathrm{DS}},G'_{\mathrm{DS}}],
\]
not the false statement that Cartan currents are
\(Q_{\mathrm{CS}}\)-exact in the unreduced holomorphic
Chern--Simons bulk.
\end{maintheorem}

\begin{maintheorem}[Topologisation for free PVAs;
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:E3-topological-free-PVA}},
under the KZ analytic SDR and topological-lift hypotheses]
\label{thm:intro-e3-free-PVA}
For finite-type freely generated finite-jet PVAs with conformal vector

\end{maintheorem}

Heisenberg and lattice VOAs reach $E_3$-topological as abelian

\paragraph{Three pillars.}

\smallskip\textbf{(1) Convergence (Stirling dominance).}
Shadow-tower coefficients satisfy
$\limsup_{r \to \infty}(|S_r(\cA)|/r!)^{1/r} = 0$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro},
Theorem~\ref{thm:wn-tempered-all-N},

$(r!)^{1/r} \sim r/e$ grows without bound, finite Arnold coupling
$\beta_\cA$ forces $|S_r(\cA)|^{1/r} \le \beta_\cA/|c|$, quotient
$\to 0$. Stirling dominates polynomial growth; ordinary
generating series $\sum_r S_r(\cA)\,\rho^r$ converges absolutely
for $\rho < \rho_*(\cA)$. Finite principal $\cW_N$ tempering does not
use the exact value of $\beta_N$; the harmonic law
$\beta_N = 12(H_N - 1)$ is
Conjecture~\ref{conj:beta-N-harmonic-closed-form} and supplies
the exact radius only when the harmonic law is assumed.

\smallskip\textbf{(2) Rigidity (finite amplitude data + refinement).}
$\mathrm{Zhu}(\cA)$ records the finite algebraic shadow on
rational/lisse rows; it does not by itself bound Massey products
through all bar degrees.  Tempering is proved only where a
family-specific arity-growth bound is supplied: finite-depth transfer,
Virasoro recurrence, finite-envelope estimates, or orbifold BV descent.
The logarithmic triplet $\cW(p)$ remains conjectural until the regular
and logarithmic amplitude bounds of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp} are proved.
Refinement is necessary: witness $K(\mathfrak{sl}_2, \sqrt{2})$
has infinite Zhu yet tempered shadow growth
(Proposition~\ref{prop:zhu-unbounded-tempered-nontrivial});
Heisenberg branching of coset modules polynomial in charge
(Proposition~\ref{prop:heisenberg-branching-polynomial}).
Correct criterion: \emph{VSKR + BGG}
(Corollary~\ref{cor:tempered-criterion-refined}), strictly weaker
than $C_2$-cofiniteness. Unbounded Zhu is not an obstruction to tempering
(Proposition~\ref{prop:zhu-unbounded-tempered-nontrivial}):
the converse of the tempered-unbounded-Zhu implication fails.
