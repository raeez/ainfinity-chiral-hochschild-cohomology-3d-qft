The characteristic bundle
\[
 \cQ(\cA) \;:=\; \{Q_g(\cA)\}_{(g,n)} \longrightarrow
 \overline{\cM}_{g,n}
\]
assigns to each stable marked curve the genus-$g$ characteristic
function of a chirally Koszul $E_1$-chiral algebra $\cA$. At each
$(g,n)$ the pair $(\cQ(\cA), \cQ(\cA^!))$ has a genus-wise Vol~I
complementarity pairing; after the total gluing datum is installed it
sits in the shifted symplectic structure below. The degree-two scalar
curvature is
$\kappaChHodge(\cA)c_1(\mathbb E)$, and the uniform-weight scalar
coefficient is
$F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)
=\kappaChHodge(\cA)\lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$. In all weights the
numerical free energy is
$F_g(\cA)=F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)
+\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}(\cA)$, where
$\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the Faber--Pandharipande numerical
trace of the off-diagonal tensor curvature.

data are installed, the total bundle carries a
$-(3g{-}3{+}n)$-shifted symplectic structure whose diagonal section is
the Koszul pair glued along clutching (Theorem~C,
\S\ref{sec:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic-native}). After the
channel propagator package is installed, the curvature promotes to a tensor
$K \in \operatorname{Sym}^2(\cF^\vee) \otimes \Omega^2(\overline{\cM}_{g,n})$

scalar and whose off-diagonal has Faber--Pandharipande trace

\S\ref{sec:theoremD-tensor-arakelov-native}). The Vol~I genus-wise
inputs are the scalar diagonal; the global shifted-symplectic and
tensor statements additionally require the data displayed below.

\section{Theorem C: total shifted-symplectic structure on $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$}
\label{sec:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic-native}

The Koszul conductor
$\rho_K(\cA) := \kappaChHodge(\cA) + \kappaChHodge(\cA^!)$ is a
scalar determined by $\cA$: it equals $0$ on Heisenberg, affine
Kac--Moody, and free fields; $13$ on Virasoro at the Koszul
self-dual point; family-specific elsewhere (Vol~I landscape
census). Vol~I, Theorem~\ref*{thm:theorem-C} establishes at each
genus $g$ separately the Lagrangian complementarity
$\kappaChHodge(\cA) + \kappaChHodge(\cA^!) = \rho_K$ and the
orthogonality $Q_g(\cA) \oplus Q_g(\cA^!) = \rho_K$. The
genus-wise identity glues to a global section on the
Deligne--Mumford modular stack $\overline{\cM}_{g,n}$ with
clutching compatibility across boundary strata.

\begin{remark}[Two Vol~I anchors under the Koszul hypothesis]
\label{rem:theoremC-A-vs-B-signpost}
The hypothesis ``chirally Koszul $E_1$-chiral algebra'' loaded
into Theorem~\ref{thm:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:theoremD-tensor-arakelov} is the
Koszul-effectiveness condition $\effKoszul$ (Type~$\varepsilon$).
It joins two Vol~I anchors. \emph{Vol~I Theorem~A} (bar--cobar
inversion $\Omega(\barB(\cA)) \xrightarrow{\sim} \cA$ on the
Koszul locus) supplies a well-defined Koszul dual~$\cA^!$ and the
Lagrangian complementarity
$\kappaChHodge(\cA) + \kappaChHodge(\cA^!) = \rho_K$.
\emph{Vol~I Theorem~B} (chiral Positselski coderived equivalence,
extending~\cite{Positselski11}) promotes the quadratic
Maurer--Cartan injection \eqref{eq:GLZ_MC_injection} of
Theorem~\ref{thm:GLZ_MC} to a bijection on the Koszul-effective
locus. Theorem~A controls the underlying object $\cA^!$, whence
the orthogonality and the shifted-symplectic structure of
Theorem~\ref{thm:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic}; Theorem~B
controls the comparison functor making the bar classifier
surjective onto $\MC(\cA^! \otimes \cB)$. The bar--cobar review
(Remark~\ref{rem:GLZ_MC_eff_caveat} of
Section~\ref{sec:bar_cobar}) records the
injection-versus-bijection distinction; Construction Problem~4
(chiral Positselski) extends Theorem~B to chiral generality. The
C/D theorems below take ``chirally Koszul $E_1$-chiral'' as the
joint hypothesis under which both anchors hold.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Total Theorem C gluing datum]
\label{def:theoremC-total-gluing-datum}
Let \(\mathcal A\) be a chirally Koszul \(E_1\)-chiral algebra on a
smooth projective curve.  A \emph{total Theorem C gluing datum}
\[
\mathfrak s_{\mathcal A}^{C}
=
\bigl(\mathcal Q_{g,n}(\mathcal A),\mathcal Q_{g,n}(\mathcal A^!),
\langle-,-\rangle_{\mathrm{Verd}},
\theta_{g,n}^{\mathrm{PTVV}},
\{\Lambda_\xi\}_\xi,\sigma_{\mathcal A}\bigr)
\]
consists of the following data for every stable pair \((g,n)\):
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=1.8em]
\item perfect derived stacks or perfect complexes
\(\mathcal Q_{g,n}(\mathcal A)\) and
\(\mathcal Q_{g,n}(\mathcal A^!)\) over
\(\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}\), carrying the characteristic functions
\(Q_g(\mathcal A)\) and \(Q_g(\mathcal A^!)\);
\item a Verdier-duality pairing on the fibres, non-degenerate on the
Koszul-effective locus, whose scalar trace is the Vol~I Theorem~C
pairing
\(\kappaChHodge(\mathcal A)+\kappaChHodge(\mathcal A^!)=\rho_K\);
\item an orientation/Mumford integration class
\(\theta_{g,n}^{\mathrm{PTVV}}\) giving the PTVV transgression
\[
\int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g,n}}
\langle-,-\rangle_{\mathrm{Verd}}\wedge
\theta_{g,n}^{\mathrm{PTVV}}
\]
as a closed \( -(3g-3+n)\)-shifted two-form;
\item for each separating or nonseparating clutching morphism \(\xi\),
a Lagrangian correspondence \(\Lambda_\xi\) identifying the pullback of
the two-form with the external product of the lower-genus two-forms and
the zero-mode contraction at the node;
\item a diagonal section \(\sigma_{\mathcal A}\) whose fibrewise image
is the Vol~I Theorem~C Lagrangian complement and whose restrictions
are compatible with all \(\Lambda_\xi\).
\end{enumerate}
The datum is not determined by the scalar identity alone; it supplies
the orientation, closedness, and clutching compatibilities needed to
apply PTVV globally.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Total shifted-symplectic consequence of Theorem~C;
\ClaimStatusConditional; licensing tags $\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\epsilon$]
\label{thm:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic}
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a chirally Koszul $E_1$-chiral algebra on a
smooth projective curve and $\mathcal{A}^!$ its Koszul dual. Suppose a
total Theorem C gluing datum
\(\mathfrak s_{\mathcal A}^{C}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:theoremC-total-gluing-datum} is supplied. Form
the relative characteristic bundle
\[
 \mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}) \;:=\;
 \bigl\{Q_g(\mathcal{A})\bigr\}_{(g,n)} \longrightarrow
 \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}
\]
associating to a stable marked curve the genus-$g$ characteristic
function. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item\label{tsc-i} The total space
 $\mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}) \oplus \mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}^!)$
 carries a canonical $-(3g{-}3{+}n)$-shifted symplectic structure
 $\omega^{\mathrm{tot}}$ in the PTVV
 sense~\cite{PTVV13}, induced by the Verdier pairing on chiral
 homology integrated against the Mumford class on
 $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$.
\item\label{tsc-ii} The diagonal section
 $\sigma_{\mathcal{A}}\colon
 \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n} \longrightarrow
 \mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}) \oplus \mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{A}^!),
 \quad
 (C, p_\bullet) \mapsto
 \bigl(Q_g(\mathcal{A})|_{(C,p_\bullet)},\,
 Q_g(\mathcal{A}^!)|_{(C,p_\bullet)}\bigr)$
 is a Lagrangian section of $\omega^{\mathrm{tot}}$: its image is a
 Lagrangian sub-stack at every stable point.
\item\label{tsc-iii} Across a boundary divisor
 $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g-1,n+2} \hookrightarrow
 \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$ (nonseparating node) or
 $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g_1,n_1+1} \times
 \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g_2,n_2+1}
 \hookrightarrow \overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$ (separating node),
 the section $\sigma_{\mathcal{A}}$ restricts compatibly with the
 clutching morphism: the stratum-wise Lagrangian complementarity
 of Theorem~C glues to a single Lagrangian section on the whole
 modular stack.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Definition~\ref{def:theoremC-total-gluing-datum}, the Verdier
pairing is perfect on the Koszul-effective locus and the class
\(\theta_{g,n}^{\mathrm{PTVV}}\) makes its transgression a closed
two-form of degree \(-(3g-3+n)\).  PTVV integration
\cite[Thm.~2.5]{PTVV13} therefore produces the shifted symplectic form
\(\omega^{\mathrm{tot}}\) on
\(\mathcal Q(\mathcal A)\oplus\mathcal Q(\mathcal A^!)\).  The
fibrewise Lagrangian condition for \(\sigma_{\mathcal A}\) is exactly
the Vol~I Theorem~C complementarity clause included in
\(\mathfrak s_{\mathcal A}^{C}\).  For a clutching morphism \(\xi\),
the Lagrangian correspondence \(\Lambda_\xi\) identifies the pulled
back two-form with the external product of the lower-genus forms and
the node zero-mode contraction.  The stated compatibility of
\(\sigma_{\mathcal A}\) with all \(\Lambda_\xi\) is precisely the
assertion that these fibrewise Lagrangians glue to one section over
the whole modular stack.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Heisenberg: Lagrangian section as Mumford class]
\label{ex:theoremC-heisenberg}
For $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{H}_k$ Heisenberg at level $k$,
the connected Hodge class is
$Q_g(\mathcal{H}_k)=k\,\omega_g$. The chiral Koszul dual is
$\Sym^{\mathrm{ch}}(V^*)$; the sign-flipped Heisenberg algebra
$\mathcal{H}_{-k}$ is the open-colour comparison model, not the
chiral dual. On the scalar comparison line the Lagrangian section is
$\sigma_{\mathcal{H}_k}(C)=(k\omega_g,-k\omega_g)$, and the
Koszul conductor is
$\rho_K(\mathcal{H}_k)=k+(-k)=0$.
At genus~$g$, the total two-form restricts to
\[
 d(k\omega_g)\wedge d(-k\omega_g)=0
\]
on the one-dimensional $(k,-k)$ diagonal. The connected scalar free
energy is
$F_g(\mathcal{H}_k)=k\,\lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}[Identification of the PTVV-symplectic ambient]
\label{rem:theoremC-ambient-identification}
Three ambients carry ``total $-(3g{-}3)$-shifted symplectic''
structures: (A) the modular stack $\overline{\cM}_{g,n}$;
(B) the derived moduli of stable maps
$\overline{\cM}_{g,n}(X, \beta)$ to an auxiliary target $X$;
(C) the total bundle
$\cQ(\cA) \oplus \cQ(\cA^!) \to \overline{\cM}_{g,n}$. Only (C)
applies. The modular stack (A) is Deligne--Mumford; its tangent
complex sits in degrees $[0,1]$ with no negative part, outside the
PTVV locus \cite[Rem.~2.7]{PTVV13}. The stable-map moduli (B)
depends on an ambient target $X$; the characteristic bundle (C) is
canonical in $\cA$ alone. The shift is $-(3g{-}3{+}n)$, not
$-(3g{-}3)$: the point markings contribute through the tangent
bundle of $\overline{\cM}_{g,n}$, of dimension $3g-3+n$.
\end{remark}

\section{Theorem D: tensor-Arakelov curvature $K \in \mathrm{Sym}^2(\cF^\vee) \otimes \Omega^2(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n})$}
\label{sec:theoremD-tensor-arakelov-native}

Let $\cA = \bigoplus_{w \ge 0} \cA_w$ be the conformal-weight
decomposition. The weight-bundle
\[
 \cF \;:=\; \bigoplus_w \cF_w \longrightarrow \overline{\cM}_{g,n},
 \qquad
 \cF_w = \text{local system of weight-$w$ conformal blocks},
\]
is finite-dimensional at each weight by $C_2$-cofiniteness
\cite{Arakawa2012C2}. The scalar Mumford--Arakelov formula
$d^2 = \kappaChHodge(\cA) \cdot \omega_g$ on the uniform-weight
locus is the weight-preserving diagonal of the modular bar
differential; the Faber--Pandharipande numerical cross-channel
correction $\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}$ is the trace of the
weight-shifting off-diagonal.
A single $\operatorname{Sym}^2$-valued tensor on $\cF$ joins the
two.

\begin{definition}[Tensor-Arakelov graph datum]
\label{def:tensor-arakelov-graph-datum}
Let \(\mathcal A=\bigoplus_{w\ge0}\mathcal A_w\) be a chirally
Koszul \(E_1\)-chiral algebra with finite weight-bounded conformal
block local systems on the stable stratum under consideration.  A
\emph{tensor-Arakelov graph datum}
\[
\mathfrak k_{\mathcal A}^{D}
=
\bigl(\mathcal F,\{P_{\alpha\beta}\},\{C_v\},
\{\mathsf G^{\mathrm{st}}_{g,2}(\alpha,\beta)\},
\operatorname{or}_{\Gamma},\tau_{\mathrm{FP}}\bigr)
\]
consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=1.8em]
\item the graded finite-rank weight bundle
\(\mathcal F=\bigoplus_w\mathcal F_w\) over the chosen stable stratum;
\item channel propagators \(P_{\alpha\beta}\) for every ordered pair of
homogeneous conformal-block basis elements, compatible with the
Arakelov Green kernel on the diagonal weight lanes;
\item local chiral OPE/CohFT vertex tensors \(C_v\), including their
Koszul signs and weight labels;
\item stable two-leg graph sets
\(\mathsf G^{\mathrm{st}}_{g,2}(\alpha,\beta)\) with orientations
\(\operatorname{or}_{\Gamma}\), automorphism factors, and convergence
or regularisation data for the displayed integrals;
\item a Faber--Pandharipande trace functional \(\tau_{\mathrm{FP}}\)
on the resulting tensor curvature, agreeing on the diagonal with the
Vol~I scalar Mumford--Arakelov trace and recording the off-diagonal
cross-channel numerical correction.
\end{enumerate}
The datum includes the finite-weight ambient and the non-scalar
channel splitting.  The scalar identity
\(\operatorname{tr}_{\mathcal F}K=\kappaChHodge(\mathcal A)\omega_g\)
does not determine it.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Tensor-Arakelov consequence of Theorem~D;
\ClaimStatusConditional; licensing tags $\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\epsilon$]
\label{thm:theoremD-tensor-arakelov}
Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a chirally Koszul $E_1$-chiral algebra with
weight decomposition $\mathcal{A} = \bigoplus_{w \ge 0}
\mathcal{A}_w$, and let
$\mathcal{F} := \bigoplus_w \mathcal{F}_w \to
\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$ be the induced weight-bundle on the
DM modular stack, with $\mathcal{F}_w$ the local system of
weight-$w$ conformal blocks. Suppose a tensor-Arakelov graph datum
\(\mathfrak k_{\mathcal A}^{D}\) of
Definition~\ref{def:tensor-arakelov-graph-datum} is supplied.  Then
the curvature of the modular bar differential is the tensor-valued
\(2\)-form
\[
 K(\mathcal{A}) \;\in\;
 \operatorname{Sym}^2(\mathcal{F}^\vee)
 \otimes \Omega^2(\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}),
\]
with the following matrix components. Choose a homogeneous basis
$\{e_\alpha\}$ of $\cF$ and write
\[
 K(\cA)=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}K_{\alpha\beta}(\cA)\,e^\alpha e^\beta .
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tensor-arakelov-component-graph-integral}
 K_{\alpha\beta}(\cA)
 =
 \sum_{\Gamma\in\mathsf{G}^{\mathrm{st}}_{g,2}(\alpha,\beta)}
 \frac{1}{|\Aut\Gamma|}
 \int_{[\overline{\cM}_\Gamma]}
 \left(\prod_{e\in E(\Gamma)}P_{\alpha_e\beta_e}\right)
 \left(\prod_{v\in V(\Gamma)}C_v\right).
\end{equation}
Here $\mathsf{G}^{\mathrm{st}}_{g,2}(\alpha,\beta)$ is the stable
two-leg graph set with external labels $e_\alpha,e_\beta$,
$P_{\alpha_e\beta_e}$ is the Arakelov propagator in the two fields
carried by the half-edges of $e$, and $C_v$ is the local chiral
OPE/CohFT vertex tensor at $v$. The scalar curvature is the trace
\[
 \operatorname{tr}_{\cF}K(\cA)
 =
 \sum_\alpha K_{\alpha\alpha}(\cA)
 =
 \kappaChHodge(\cA)\,\omega_g.
\]
The entries satisfy
\begin{enumerate}
\item\label{td-i} \emph{Diagonal (UNIFORM-WEIGHT) entries.}
 $K_{w,w} = \kappa_w(\mathcal{A}) \cdot \omega_g^{(w)}$, where
 $\kappa_w$ is the weight-$w$ Arakelov anomaly coefficient and
 $\omega_g^{(w)}$ is the weight-$w$-twisted Arakelov form on
 $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$ (Faltings' Arakelov metric on
 $\det \mathcal{F}_w$). The scalar Vol~I formula
 $d^2=\kappaChHodge(\mathcal A)\omega_g$ is the total trace
 $\operatorname{tr}_\mathcal{F}(K) = \sum_w K_{w,w}$.
\item\label{td-ii} \emph{Off-diagonal (cross-channel) entries.}
 For $w_1 \ne w_2$, $K_{w_1,w_2}$ is a genuinely tensorial
 $2$-form. Its Faber--Pandharipande numerical trace contributes
 $\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}|_{w_1,w_2}$ on the
 \textsc{all-weight} multi-sector stratum and vanishes on
 \textsc{uniform-weight}.
\item\label{td-iii} \emph{Symmetry.}
 $K_{w_1, w_2} = K_{w_2, w_1}$ (bosonic sector) or
 $(-1)^{|w_1||w_2|} K_{w_2, w_1}$ (super-graded sector), so
 $K(\mathcal{A})$ lands in $\operatorname{Sym}^2$ or
 $\operatorname{Sym}^2_{\mathrm{super}}$ accordingly.
\item\label{td-iv} \emph{Tensor chiral Mumford formula.}
$
 \int_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}} \operatorname{tr}_\mathcal{F}
 \bigl(\exp(K(\mathcal{A}))\bigr)
 \;=\;
 \prod_w
 \int_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}} \exp(\kappa_w \cdot
 \omega_g^{(w)})
 \;+\; \delta^{\mathrm{cross}}_g,
$
\noindent
generalising Mumford's scalar formula; the first factor is the
weight-diagonal contribution, $\delta^{\mathrm{cross}}_g$ the
cross-channel correction from the off-diagonal entries.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The datum \(\mathfrak k_{\mathcal A}^{D}\) supplies the finite
weight-bundle, the channel propagators, the vertex tensors, the
oriented stable graph sets, and the Faber--Pandharipande trace
functional.  Decompose the modular bar differential by source and
target weights,
\[
d=\sum_{w_1,w_2}d_{w_1\to w_2}.
\]
The weight-\((w_1,w_3)\) component of \(d^2\) is
\[
\sum_{w_2} d_{w_2\to w_3}d_{w_1\to w_2},
\]
and therefore lands in
\(\mathcal F_{w_1}^{\vee}\otimes \mathcal F_{w_3}^{\vee}\).  Expanding
these two contractions with the graph datum gives
\eqref{eq:tensor-arakelov-component-graph-integral}: propagators label
edges, OPE/CohFT tensors label vertices, and
\(|\Aut\Gamma|^{-1}\operatorname{or}_{\Gamma}\) gives the
Getzler--Kapranov symmetry factor.

On a diagonal weight lane the compatibility of \(P_{\alpha\beta}\) with
the Arakelov Green kernel identifies \(K_{w,w}\) with
\(\kappa_w(\mathcal A)\omega_g^{(w)}\), and the trace over \(w\)
recovers the Vol~I scalar formula.  On an off-diagonal lane the same
graph expansion defines the tensor entry \(K_{w_1,w_2}\); applying
\(\tau_{\mathrm{FP}}\) gives the numerical cross-channel correction
\(\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}|_{w_1,w_2}\).  The symmetry statement is
the Koszul symmetry built into the oriented graph datum.  Finally,
exponentiating \(K\), separating diagonal factors from off-diagonal
entries, and applying \(\tau_{\mathrm{FP}}\) gives the displayed tensor
Mumford formula.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Identification of the weight-bundle ambient
 $\mathcal{F}$]
\label{rem:theoremD-ambient-identification}
Three ambients carry tensor targets
$\operatorname{Sym}^2(F^\vee) \otimes \Omega^2$:
(A) the Hodge bundle $\Lambda_g$ of rank $g$;
(B) the $\Lambda$-twisted tensor ladder
$\bigotimes_k \Lambda_g^{\otimes k}$, an infinite sum without
finite-rank structure;
(C) the weight-bundle
$\mathcal{F} = \bigoplus_w \mathcal{F}_w$. Only (C) carries the
rank structure $K$ demands: (A) of rank $g$ is too coarse for the
weight separation visible to the bar differential; (B) has no
finite rank at each degree. On admissible/lisse simple-quotient
surfaces Arakawa $C_2$-cofiniteness gives finite-dimensional
Li-associated pieces; on generic class-$\mathbf M$ surfaces the finite
input is the PBW/strong-generation finite-weight profile, not a
$C_2$-cofiniteness assertion.  With the appropriate finite input fixed,
the Li-filtration weight-truncation makes
\(\bigoplus_w \mathcal{F}_w\) a graded local system of finite total rank
over each stable stratum.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Family-specific weight distribution of the
 diagonal trace]
\label{prop:theoremD-weight-distribution}
\ClaimStatusProvedHere
The identification
$\operatorname{tr}_\mathcal{F}(K) = \kappaChHodge(\cA) \cdot \omega_g$
holds across the Vol~I landscape; the per-weight decomposition
$\kappa_w$ is family-specific:
\begin{itemize}
\item Heisenberg $\mathcal{H}_k$ (single strong generator $J$ at
 weight~$1$): $\kappa_1 = k$, all other $\kappa_w = 0$;
 $\operatorname{tr}(K) = k = \kappaChHodge(\mathcal{H}_k)$.
\item Virasoro $\mathrm{Vir}_c$ (single strong generator $T$ at
 weight~$2$): $\kappa_2 = c/2$, all other $\kappa_w = 0$;
 $\operatorname{tr}(K) = c/2 = \kappaChHodge(\mathrm{Vir}_c)$.
\item $\mathcal{W}_N$ (strong generators at weights
 $2, 3, \ldots, N$): $\kappa_j = c/j$ for
 $j \in \{2, \ldots, N\}$, all other $\kappa_w = 0$;
 $\operatorname{tr}(K) = \sum_{j=2}^N c/j = c(H_N - 1) =
 \kappaChHodge(\mathcal{W}_N)$.
\end{itemize}
Virasoro refutes a uniform linear-in-weight law: a linear
distribution would predict $\kappa_2 = c$, in conflict with
$\kappaChHodge(\mathrm{Vir}_c) = c/2$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof]
Heisenberg and Virasoro each have a single strong generator;
single-weight support follows from the chiral bar differential
vanishing on weight cohomology outside the generator's weight. The
principal $\mathcal{W}_N$ generates strong conformal primaries at
weights $2, 3, \ldots, N$ (Fateev--Lukyanov), each contributing
$c/j$ through the Zamolodchikov OPE at weight $j$; the harmonic
sum $\sum_{j=2}^N c/j$ recovers the Vol~I kappa formula
$\kappaChHodge(\mathcal{W}_N) = c(H_N - 1)$ with
$H_N = \sum_{j=1}^N 1/j$ (C4 of the Vol~I census). Substituting
$\kappa_w = \alpha w$ into Virasoro gives $\kappa_2 = 2\alpha$;
solving against $\kappaChHodge(\mathrm{Vir}_c) = c/2$ yields
$\alpha = c/4$, whence $\kappa_3 = 3c/4 \neq 0$, in conflict with
single-generator support.
\end{proof}

\section{Corollaries and cross-volume coherence}
\label{sec:theorems-C-D-cross-volume}

The Vol~II-native total forms contain Vol~I's genus-wise statements
as partial-to-total restrictions:

\begin{itemize}
 \item Vol~I Theorem~\ref*{thm:theorem-C} (genus-wise Lagrangian)
 restricts Theorem~C to each stable stratum of
 $\overline{\cM}_{g,n}$; the total Lagrangian glues genus-wise
 sections along clutching morphisms
 (Theorem~\ref{thm:theoremC-total-shifted-symplectic}(iii)).
 \item Vol~I Theorem~\ref*{thm:theorem-D} (uniform-weight scalar
 $F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)
 =\kappaChHodge(\cA)\lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$) is the total diagonal trace
 $\operatorname{tr}_\cF(K) = \sum_w \kappa_w(\cA) \cdot
 \omega_g^{(w)}$ collapsing to
 $F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)$ by the family-specific
 distribution law
 (Proposition~\ref{prop:theoremD-weight-distribution}); the
 Faber--Pandharipande trace of the off-diagonal tensor curvature gives
 $\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}$, the cross-channel couplings at
 $w_1 \neq w_2$, computed for $W_3$ at
 $g = 2$ in Chapter~\ref{ch:theory-hochschild}.
\end{itemize}

Section~C supplies the data needed to pass from the genus-wise Vol~I

promote the scalar diagonal to tensor curvature on the weight-bundle.

compatibility and recover the Vol~I scalar form by specialisation.  The
characteristic-datum package is therefore a precise tensor-level
obligation rather than a consequence of the scalar trace alone.
