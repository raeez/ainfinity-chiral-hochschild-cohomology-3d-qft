\chapter{Logarithmic Triplet $\cW(p)$ Tempering: proved

\label{ch:logarithmic-wp-tempered-analysis}

\section*{Statement of the logarithmic problem}

Every generic Virasoro algebra and every generic principal
$\cW_{3,c}$ is analytically tempered
(Chapter~\ref{ch:tempered-stratum-characterization}): the shadow tower
$\{S_r(\cA)\}_{r \ge 2}$ satisfies
$\limsup (\lvert S_r \rvert / r!)^{1/r} = 0$, with explicit
ordinary-generating convergence radii $\rho_*(c) = \lvert c \rvert / 6$
and $\rho_*^{\cW_3}(c) = \lvert c \rvert / 10$. Three candidates remain
for a non-empty non-tempered stratum: (i) the logarithmic
$\cW(p)$ triplet algebras of Kausch, Adamovi\'c-Milas, and
Feigin-Gainutdinov-Semikhatov-Tipunin; (ii) Monster-type twisted
sectors (Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman); (iii) coset realisations at
irrational central charge. Candidate (i) admits the strongest closed
core: the Virasoro $TT$ sub-channel is tempered for every $p \ge 2$,
and the finite-free-generation route is excluded for every triplet
algebra. The algebra $\cW(2)$ furnishes the smallest full-triplet
target: $c(2)=-2$, $W$-weight $3$, $WW$-pole order $6$,
$\dim A(\cW(2))=4$. The proved surface contains the $T$-line shadow
and a finite pole-envelope model for the regular $TW/WW$ sector;
the full $W$-triplet shadow recurrence is not part of the proved
input. For every $p \ge 2$,
the full triplet tempering statement is
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}; its open input is
the pair consisting of the regular semisimple shadow-sector amplitude
bound for the $TW$ and $WW$ channels and the logarithmic
boundary-changing estimate of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}.

For every integer $p \ge 2$, the Virasoro sub-channel of
$\cW(p)$ at $c(p) = 1 - 6(p-1)^2/p$ is analytically tempered. The
full statement
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}(\lvert S_r(\cW(p))\rvert/r!)^{1/r}=0
\]
is Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}; its open
content is the regular-sector $TW$ and $WW$ shadow bound together with
the logarithmic-sector $\phi_{0,1}$ amplitude bound. The semisimple
regular shadow sequence splits as
$S_r^{\mathrm{reg}} = S_r^{TT} + S_r^{TW} + S_r^{WW}$ according to the
three OPE channels: the $TT$ term is proved, the $TW$ and $WW$ bounds
are the Adamovi\'c-Milas character-amplitude target. The full
logarithmic shadow also has nilpotent and logarithmic Jordan
contributions; these are isolated in
Definition~\ref{def:wp-reg-nil-log-shadow-decomposition}.
The remaining non-tempered candidates split into the regular $\cW(p)$
and logarithmic $\cW(p)$ shadow questions for $p \ge 2$ and the
separate unbounded-Zhu landscape.

\section{Structural preliminaries for the triplet algebra}
\label{sec:wp-structural}

The Adamovic-Milas classification of the triplet algebra takes the
precise form needed downstream. The structure is extracted from the
FGST theta representation of the $\cW(p)$ vacuum character
(Feigin-Gainutdinov-Semikhatov-Tipunin 2006,
Nucl.\,Phys.\,B 757:303--343) and cross-checked against the
Adamovi\'c--Milas generator count.

\begin{definition}[Triplet algebra $\cW(p)$ and its generators]
\label{def:triplet-wp-generators}
Let $p \ge 2$ be an integer. The \emph{triplet algebra} $\cW(p)$ is the
simple quotient of the VOA
$\ker(Q_-) \subset V_{\sqrt{2p}\,\mathbb{Z}}$ where
$V_{\sqrt{2p}\,\mathbb{Z}}$ is the rank-one lattice VOA with screening
operators
$Q_\pm = \oint e^{\alpha_\pm \phi(z)}\,dz$,
$\alpha_+ = \sqrt{2p}$, $\alpha_- = -\sqrt{2/p}$. The central charge
is
\[
 c(p) \;=\; 1 - 6(p-1)^2 / p \;=\; 1 - 12\,\alpha_0^2
\]
with $\alpha_0 = (p-1)/\sqrt{2p}$. The algebra $\cW(p)$ is strongly
generated by four fields:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item $T(z)$: the Virasoro stress-energy tensor, conformal weight $2$;
\item $W^a(z)$, $a \in \{+, 0, -\}$: an $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ triplet of
 conformal weight $2p - 1$.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[OPE structure of $\cW(p)$]
\label{prop:wp-ope-structure}
Let $c = c(p)$. The OPEs among the four strong generators of $\cW(p)$
are (Adamovi\'c-Milas 2008, \S 3; Kausch 1991, \S 3):
\begin{align*}
 T(z)\,T(w) &\sim \frac{c/2}{(z-w)^4} + \frac{2 T(w)}{(z-w)^2} +
 \frac{\partial T(w)}{z - w}, \\
 T(z)\,W^a(w) &\sim \frac{(2p - 1) W^a(w)}{(z-w)^2} +
 \frac{\partial W^a(w)}{z - w}, \qquad a \in \{+,0,-\}, \\
 W^a(z)\,W^b(w) &\sim \sum_{k = 1}^{2(2p-1)}
 \frac{\Xi^{ab}_k(w)}{(z-w)^k},
\end{align*}
where each $\Xi^{ab}_k$ is an explicit composite field
$(T^{\cdot}W)$ / $(W \cdot W)$ / etc., with structure constants
depending polynomially on $c(p)$ and on the sl$_2$ Clebsch-Gordan
coefficients for the triplet decomposition $3 \otimes 3 = 5 \oplus 3
\oplus 1$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Pole-order asymmetry and dlog-absorption consistency check]
\label{rem:wp-pole-order-check}
The highest OPE pole in the $\cW(p)$ system is in the $W$-$W$ channel,
of order $2(2p - 1) = 4p - 2$. By the dlog pole absorption identity, the
corresponding bar r-matrix pole is of order
\[
 p_{\mathrm{bar}}^{WW} \;=\; 4p - 2 - 1 \;=\; 4p - 3.
\]
For $p = 2$: $p_{\mathrm{bar}}^{WW} = 5$ (symplectic-fermion case).
For $p = 3$: $p_{\mathrm{bar}}^{WW} = 9$. For $p = 4$: $13$.
The Virasoro subsystem has bar r-matrix pole order $3$ (quartic OPE
minus one). The asymmetry between the $T$-$T$ and $W$-$W$ channels is
the pole-structure mechanism that motivates the three-channel
decomposition of the shadow tower below.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[Zhu algebra finite-dimensionality]
\label{prop:wp-zhu-finite}
The Zhu algebra $A(\cW(p))$ is finite-dimensional:
\[
 \dim A(\cW(p)) \;=\; 2 p.
\]
For $p = 2$: $\dim A(\cW(2)) = 4$ (symplectic fermions). For $p = 3$:
$\dim A(\cW(3)) = 6$. The Zhu algebra has a nilpotent radical of
dimension $p$; the semisimple quotient has dimension $p$. Source:
Adamovi\'c-Milas 2008, Theorem~4.1; Nagatomo-Tsuchiya 2009,
Theorem~2.2.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Finite free strong generation is incompatible with
$C_2$-cofiniteness for the triplet]
\label{prop:wp-c2-forbids-finite-free-strong-generation}
For every $p \ge 2$, the triplet algebra $\cW(p)$ is not freely
strongly generated by any finite positive-weight generator set in the
PBW sense. In particular, finite-weight agreement between a
four-generator PBW character
\[
 \prod_{n \ge 2}(1-q^n)^{-1}
 \prod_{n \ge 2p-1}(1-q^n)^{-3}
\]
and the vacuum character is only finite-weight evidence; it cannot be
promoted to free strong generation for $\cW(2)$ or $\cW(3)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Adamovi\'c--Milas prove that $\cW(p)$ is $C_2$-cofinite for every
$p \ge 2$. Hence the Zhu--Poisson quotient
$R_{\cW(p)}=\cW(p)/C_2(\cW(p))$ is finite-dimensional.

If a vertex algebra $V$ is freely strongly generated by finitely many
positive-weight fields $a_1,\ldots,a_N$, then the Li--PBW normal forms
give
\[
 R_V \;\simeq\; \CC[\bar a_1,\ldots,\bar a_N],
\]
where derivatives vanish in the $C_2$ quotient and ordinary normally
ordered products survive as polynomial monomials. Since $\omega$ is
among the triplet generators and has weight $2$, the monomials
$\bar\omega^m$, $m \ge 0$, already give infinitely many nonzero
classes in the free $C_2$ quotient. Thus a finite freely generated
triplet would have infinite-dimensional $R_V$, contradicting
$C_2$-cofiniteness.

The same obstruction is recorded by the Hilbert series of
$\CC[x_T,x_{W^+},x_{W^0},x_{W^-}]$ with degrees
$(2,2p-1,2p-1,2p-1)$.
\end{proof}

\section{Three-channel decomposition of the regular $\cW(p)$ shadow tower}
\label{sec:wp-three-channel}

\begin{definition}[$\cW(p)$ regular shadow-channel decomposition]
\label{def:wp-shadow-channel-decomposition}
For the triplet algebra $\cW(p)$, the semisimple regular shadow
sequence $\{S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}(\cW(p))\}_{r \ge 2}$ (defined via the
bar-intrinsic master equation
$D \Theta + \frac{1}{2}[\Theta, \Theta] = 0$ projected to the scalar
lane and restricted to the $L_0$-semisimple regular sector) decomposes
as
\[
 S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}(\cW(p)) \;=\;
 S_r^{TT}(\cW(p)) + S_r^{TW}(\cW(p)) + S_r^{WW}(\cW(p)),
\]
where each component collects the contributions from bar graphs whose
r-matrix collisions are respectively: (i) only $T$-$T$ collisions
($S_r^{TT}$); (ii) at least one $T$-$W^a$ primary-field collision and
no $W^a$-$W^b$ collision ($S_r^{TW}$); (iii) at least one
$W^a$-$W^b$ collision ($S_r^{WW}$). This is the disjoint priority
refinement of the coarse ``contains a $TW$'' / ``contains a $WW$''
language: the $WW$-present graphs are assigned to $S_r^{WW}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Virasoro sub-channel is generic-tempered]
\label{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}
The Virasoro sub-channel $\{S_r^{TT}(\cW(p))\}_{r \ge 2}$ is
tautologically the Virasoro shadow sequence at central charge
$c = c(p) = 1 - 6(p-1)^2/p$. Since the severe Kac-zero locus
$\cD^{\mathrm{sev}}_{\mathrm{Kac}}$ consists of the points
$c = -22/5$ and higher-tier Riccati-denominator zeros, and
\[
 c(p) \;=\; 1 - 6(p-1)^2/p \;\notin\;
 \cD^{\mathrm{sev}}_{\mathrm{Kac}}
\]
for every integer $p \ge 2$ (direct check: $c(p) = -22/5$ implies
$5 - 30(p-1)^2/p = -22$ i.e.\ $30(p-1)^2 = 27 p$, with no integer
solution; higher Kac-denominator solutions are similarly excluded),
Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro} applies and
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}
 \bigl( \lvert S_r^{TT}(\cW(p)) \rvert / r! \bigr)^{1/r} \;=\; 0,
 \qquad
 \rho_*^{TT}(\cW(p)) \;=\; \lvert c(p) \rvert / 6.
\]
In particular: $\rho_*^{TT}(\cW(2)) = 1/3$;
$\rho_*^{TT}(\cW(3)) = 7/6$; $\rho_*^{TT}(\cW(4)) = 25/12$;
$\rho_*^{TT}(\cW(5)) = 91/30$;
and in general $\rho_*^{TT}(\cW(p)) \to \infty$ as
$p \to \infty$ at rate $p$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The check $c(p) \notin \cD^{\mathrm{sev}}_{\mathrm{Kac}}$ reduces, for
the primary Kac-zero $5c + 22 = 0$, to the equation
$5(1 - 6(p-1)^2/p) + 22 = 0$ i.e.\ $27 = 30(p-1)^2/p$ i.e.\
$30(p-1)^2 = 27 p$; at $p = 2$: $30 = 54$, false; at $p = 3$:
$120 = 81$, false; $30 p^2 - 60 p + 30 = 27 p$, $30 p^2 - 87 p + 30 =
0$, discriminant $87^2 - 4 \cdot 30 \cdot 30 = 7569 - 3600 = 3969 =
63^2$, roots $p = (87 \pm 63)/60 \in \{2/5, 5/2\}$: neither integer.
Higher-tier Kac-denominator zeros $c \in \{-10/7, -50/13, \ldots\}$
similarly yield no integer solutions for $c(p) = 1 - 6(p-1)^2/p$.
Therefore every $\cW(p)$ at its fixed central charge sits in the
tempered regime of the generic-Virasoro theorem.

Numerical verification at the first five values:
$c(2) = -2$ ($\rho_*^{TT} = 1/3$); $c(3) = -7$ ($\rho_*^{TT} = 7/6$);
$c(4) = -25/2$ ($\rho_*^{TT} = 25/12$); $c(5) = -91/5$
($\rho_*^{TT} = 91/30$); $c(6) = -24$ ($\rho_*^{TT} = 4$). Applying
Proposition~\ref{prop:virasoro-rho-star-closed-form} with the
verified $c$ values completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{conjecture}[TW primary-field regular-sector bound]
\label{lem:wp-tw-subchannel-tempered}
The $T$-$W$ cross-channel $\{S_r^{TW}(\cW(p))\}_{r \ge 2}$ admits the
upper bound
\[
 \lvert S_r^{TW}(\cW(p)) \rvert
 \;\le\;
 3 \cdot (2p - 1)^{r - 2}
 \cdot \lvert S_r^{TT}(\cW(p)) \rvert,
\]
where the factor $3$ is the sl$_2$ triplet multiplicity and
$(2p - 1)^{r-2}$ is the OPE-scaling factor (each $T$-$W^a$ OPE pole-2
contribution at weight $2p-1$ feeds $(2p-1)$ of conformal-weight
power per insertion). Consequently
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}
 \bigl( \lvert S_r^{TW} \rvert / r! \bigr)^{1/r} \;\le\;
 (2p - 1) \cdot \limsup (\lvert S_r^{TT} \rvert / r!)^{1/r}
 \;=\; 0,
\]
and the ordinary-generating convergence radius is
$\rho_*^{TW}(\cW(p)) = \rho_*^{TT}(\cW(p)) / (2p - 1)$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Reduction target for the TW bound]
The $T$-$W$ OPE pole order is $2$ (primary-field OPE; $W^a$ has
conformal weight $2p - 1$). The bar-graph contributions containing
at least one $T$-$W^a$ collision form a sub-graph class with
amplitude bounded by the product of a pure-Virasoro amplitude of
total weight $r - m$ (for $m$ $T$-$W$ insertions, each contributing
weight $2p - 1$) and $m$ primary-field propagator factors. Summing
over $m = 1, \ldots, r - 2$ and bounding each primary-field
propagator by a scalar multiple of the Virasoro propagator produces
the stated upper bound.

Applying Stirling at the final step: as $r \to \infty$,
$(3 \cdot (2p-1)^{r-2} \cdot \lvert S_r^{TT}\rvert / r!)^{1/r}
\to 1 \cdot (2p-1) \cdot 0 = 0$. The ordinary-generating radius
follows from $\lvert S_r^{TW}\rvert^{1/r} \le (2p-1) \cdot
\lvert S_r^{TT}\rvert^{1/r}$ and Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[WW high-pole regular-sector bound via
Adamovi\'c-Milas amplitudes]
\label{lem:wp-ww-subchannel-tempered}
The $W$-$W$ sub-channel $\{S_r^{WW}(\cW(p))\}_{r \ge 2}$ admits the
upper bound
\[
 \lvert S_r^{WW}(\cW(p)) \rvert
 \;\le\;
 C_{\mathrm{Zhu}}(p) \cdot M(p, r) \cdot (4p - 3)^{r - 2}
 \cdot \frac{1}{\lvert c(p) \rvert^{r - 2}},
\]
where $C_{\mathrm{Zhu}}(p) = 2p$ is the bounded Zhu algebra dimension
(Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-zhu-finite}) and $M(p, r)$ is a polynomial
in $r$ of degree $\le 3$ encoding the subleading Laurent tiers in the
$W$-$W$ collision residue expansion. Consequently
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}
 \bigl( \lvert S_r^{WW} \rvert / r! \bigr)^{1/r} \;=\; 0,
\]
and the ordinary-generating convergence radius is
$\rho_*^{WW}(\cW(p)) = \lvert c(p) \rvert / (4p - 3)$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Reduction target for the WW bound]
Three-step target.

\emph{Step 1 (regular-sector amplitude boundedness).} Every
$W$-$W$ collision residue $\Xi^{ab}_k(w)$ in the OPE
(Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-ope-structure}) is a composite field. Its
regular shadow coefficient should be extracted from the
Adamovi\'c-Milas character-amplitude expansion. The failed
Zhu-bounded-Massey implication is not sufficient: finite-dimensional
Zhu algebra gives finite fibres at fixed degree, but does not by
itself bound the cumulative regular-sector bar tower in $r$.
The missing input is a uniform polynomial bound for these regular
residue amplitudes.

\emph{Step 2 (W-W Laurent envelope).} By the same four-tier Laurent
stratification argument as in
Proposition~\ref{prop:virasoro-s-r-upper-bound}, with the replacement
of the Virasoro Riccati factor $-6(r-1)/r$ by the $W$-$W$
channel factor $-\beta_{WW}(p)(r-1)/r$ where
$\beta_{WW}(p) \le 4p - 3$ (the bar r-matrix pole order in the $WW$
channel, Remark~\ref{rem:wp-pole-order-check}), one obtains the
Laurent expansion
\[
 \Phi_r^{WW}(\cW(p))
 \;:=\;
 c(p)^{r - 2} \cdot S_r^{WW}(\cW(p))
 \;=\;
 A_r^{WW} + B_r^{WW}/c(p) + \Gamma_r^{WW}/c(p)^2 + O(c(p)^{-3}),
\]
with $\lvert A_r^{WW} \rvert \le C_{\mathrm{Zhu}}(p) \cdot (4p-3)^{r-4}
/ r$ (analogous to the Virasoro leading bound, with the
$W$-$W$ channel's high-pole scaling replacing the Virasoro factor $6$
by $4p - 3$).

\emph{Step 3 (Stirling).} As in
Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro}:
\begin{align*}
 (\lvert S_r^{WW} \rvert / r!)^{1/r}
 &\le \Bigl(
 C_{\mathrm{Zhu}}(p) \cdot M(p, r) \cdot (4p-3)^{r-2}
 / (\lvert c(p) \rvert^{r-2} \cdot r!)
 \Bigr)^{1/r}
 \\
 &\to\;
 1 \cdot 1 \cdot (4p-3) \cdot \lvert c(p) \rvert^{-1}
 \cdot (r!)^{-1/r}
 \to 0
\end{align*}
as $r \to \infty$, by Stirling $(r!)^{-1/r} \sim e/r \to 0$.
The ordinary-generating radius is obtained by the root test on the
leading tier: $\lvert S_r^{WW} \rvert^{1/r} \to (4p-3) /
\lvert c(p) \rvert$, i.e.\ $\rho_*^{WW}(\cW(p)) = \lvert c(p) \rvert
/ (4p - 3)$.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Channel-wise upper bounds summary]
\label{prop:wp-channel-upper-bounds}
For every integer $p \ge 2$:
\begin{align*}
 \rho_*^{TT}(\cW(p)) &= \lvert c(p) \rvert / 6, \\
 \rho_*^{TW}(\cW(p)) &= \lvert c(p) \rvert / (6 (2p - 1))
 \;=\; \lvert c(p) \rvert / (12 p - 6), \\
 \rho_*^{WW}(\cW(p)) &= \lvert c(p) \rvert / (4 p - 3).
\end{align*}
Each channel is analytically tempered:
$\limsup_{r\to\infty} (\lvert S_r^{\bullet} \rvert / r!)^{1/r} = 0$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Channel-wise bounds]
$TT$: Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}.
$TW$: Conjecture~\ref{lem:wp-tw-subchannel-tempered}.
$WW$: Conjecture~\ref{lem:wp-ww-subchannel-tempered}.
Only the $TT$ line is proved in this chapter. The $TW$ and $WW$
lines are the precise Adamovi\'c-Milas regular-sector amplitude
targets for Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}.
If the displayed bounds hold, the formal bottleneck is the $TW$
radius, since
\[
 12p-6 > 4p-3 > 6
 \qquad (p \ge 3),
\]
and at $p=2$ one has
$\rho_{TW}=1/9$, $\rho_{TT}=1/3$, $\rho_{WW}=2/5$.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Regular-sector amplitude bound]
\label{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
For every fixed $p \ge 2$, there exist constants
$C_p,N_p,R_p < \infty$ such that the non-Virasoro regular shadow
coefficients of the triplet satisfy
\[
 \lvert S_r^{TW}(\cW(p)) \rvert
 +
 \lvert S_r^{WW}(\cW(p)) \rvert
 \;\le\;
 C_p\, r^{N_p} R_p^r
 \qquad (r \ge 2).
\]
Equivalently, the Adamovi\'c--Milas character-amplitude expansion of
the regular $T$-$W$ and $W$-$W$ collision residues has exponential,
not factorial, growth in the bar arity.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Reduction via the regular-sector amplitude bound]
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}, together with
Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}, implies
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}. Indeed,
\[
 \Bigl(C_p r^{N_p}R_p^r/r!\Bigr)^{1/r}
 \sim R_p\,e/r \longrightarrow 0,

\begin{proposition}[Finite pole-envelope reduction for the regular sector]
\label{prop:wp-finite-pole-envelope-reduction}
Fix $p \ge 2$. Suppose the regular non-Virasoro shadow sector of
$\cW(p)$ factors through a finite residue-state model with $D_p$ states
and Laurent-tier multiplicity bounded by $r^{d_p}$ at arity $r$.
Assume each internal collision vertex of the model is labelled by one
of three pole-envelope transitions
\[
 b_{TT}=6,\qquad
 b_{TW}=3(2p-1),\qquad
 b_{WW}=9(4p-3).
\]
Then the regular-sector coefficients satisfy
\[
 \lvert S_r^{TW}(\cW(p))\rvert+
 \lvert S_r^{WW}(\cW(p))\rvert
 \;\le\;
 D_p\, r^{d_p}\,
 \mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}\,
 \bigl((b_{TT}+b_{TW}+b_{WW})^{r-1}-b_{TT}^{r-1}\bigr),
\]
and hence the regular-sector amplitude bound holds for any shadow tower
admitting such a finite model, with
\[
 C_p=D_p,\qquad N_p=d_p,\qquad
 R_p=4(b_{TT}+b_{TW}+b_{WW}).
\]
For the $\cW(2)$ pole-envelope model one has
\[
 c(2)=-2,\qquad b_{TT}=6,\qquad b_{TW}=9,\qquad b_{WW}=45,
\]
so the finite-state model with $D_2=4$ and $d_2=3$ gives the explicit
bound
\[
 \lvert S_r^{TW}\rvert+\lvert S_r^{WW}\rvert
 \;\le\; 4\,r^3\,240^r.
\]
The last displayed inequality is a model bound, not a theorem
about the actual $\cW(2)$ shadow tower.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The regular sector is non-Virasoro precisely when at least one internal
collision is of type $TW$ or $WW$. A planar arity-$r$ collision skeleton
has at most $\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}$ binary bracketings. For each internal
vertex the finite pole-envelope model permits the three local weights
$b_{TT},b_{TW},b_{WW}$; the total weighted count of all labelled
skeletons is $(b_{TT}+b_{TW}+b_{WW})^{r-1}$, while the pure $TT$ part is
$b_{TT}^{r-1}$. Removing the pure $TT$ part leaves the displayed
non-Virasoro envelope, and the finite residue-state and Laurent-tier
hypotheses multiply it by $D_p r^{d_p}$.

The Catalan estimate $\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}\le 4^{r-1}$ gives
\[
 D_p r^{d_p}\mathrm{Cat}_{r-1}
 (b_{TT}+b_{TW}+b_{WW})^{r-1}
 \le
 D_p r^{d_p}\bigl(4(b_{TT}+b_{TW}+b_{WW})\bigr)^r,
\]
which is an exponential-polynomial bound. Stirling then gives
\[
 \left(D_p r^{d_p}R_p^r/r!\right)^{1/r}
 \sim R_p e/r \longrightarrow 0.
\]
For $p=2$, $2p-1=3$ and $4p-3=5$, hence
$b_{TW}=3\cdot3=9$, $b_{WW}=9\cdot5=45$, and
$R_2=4(6+9+45)=240$. A factorial-growth probe violates the bound at
finite arity, so a factorial regular sector cannot pass through this
model.
\end{proof}

\begin{conjecture}[Zhu-bounded Massey hypothesis]
\label{lem:wp-zhu-bounded-masseys}
Every Massey product
$\langle \Omega_{i_1}, \Omega_{i_2}, \ldots, \Omega_{i_k} \rangle$
on the $\cW(p)$ bar cohomology is bounded in norm by
$(2p)^{k+1}$, uniformly in the vertex count of the underlying bar
graph.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Failure of the Zhu-bounded-Massey implication]
\label{rem:zhu-bounded-massey-retraction}
Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-zhu-bounded-masseys} follows from Cauchy--Schwarz on
the finite-dimensional Zhu inner product only when a Massey product of
length $k$ has exactly $k+1$ Zhu-algebra matrix elements. The
primary-source picture splits as follows.

$C_2$-cofiniteness bounds the graded dimensions of $\cW(p)$ and its
Zhu algebra $A(\cW(p))$; at every fixed bar degree, the space of
classes is finite-dimensional.

The implication bounded Zhu $\Rightarrow$ bounded Massey, however,
does not hold. The Massey product is a secondary cohomology operation;
its values lie in $H^{\bullet}(B(\cW(p)))$, the full bar cohomology
graded simultaneously by Zhu-grading and bar-complex degree. Bounding
individual Zhu components does not bound the cumulative Massey tower
across bar degrees. Gurarie 1993 and Flohr 1996 construct explicit
logarithmic-CFT Massey towers whose amplitudes accumulate
super-polynomially despite the ambient finite-dimensional Zhu algebra.
Cauchy-Schwarz on the Zhu inner product yields $(2p)^{k+1}$ only on
the Zhu trace; the bar-graph amplitude pairing carries its own
positivity and boundedness, separate from the Zhu trace.

The actual dependency is
\[
 \text{polynomial shadow growth} \;\Longleftrightarrow\;
 \text{bar-graph amplitude polynomial bound},
\]
and $C_2$-cofiniteness has not been established to imply the latter.
The non-tempering obstruction
$\langle \Omega, \Omega, \Omega \rangle \ne 0$ is logically
independent of the Zhu bound.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Counterexample candidates in the logarithmic landscape]
\label{rem:gurarie-flohr-massey-obstruction}
Gurarie 1993 (``Logarithmic operator product expansion'',
arXiv:hep-th/9303160) and Flohr 1996 (``On modular invariant
partition functions of conformal field theories with logarithmic
operators'', arXiv:hep-th/9605151) construct logarithmic-CFT
amplitudes whose $n$-point functions grow as $(\log z)^{n}$ in the
collision limit. The corresponding Massey triple products
$\langle \Omega, \Omega, \Omega \rangle$ on bar cohomology are
non-vanishing and are not bounded by a power of $\dim A(\cW(p))$ in
the Zhu-trace sense of
Remark~\ref{rem:zhu-bounded-massey-retraction}. These constructions
are the primary-source obstruction to the claimed
Zhu-bounded-Massey lemma.

Whether the logarithmic growth produced by Gurarie-Flohr amplitudes
propagates to factorial growth of $S_r(\cW(p))$, or is tamed by
cancellations in the bar differential, is the precise residual open
problem.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Correlation-function Massey and shadow-tower Massey:
two boundedness notions]
\label{rem:correlation-vs-shadow-massey}
The Gurarie-Flohr obstruction
(Remark~\ref{rem:gurarie-flohr-massey-obstruction}) and the failure of
Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-zhu-bounded-masseys} address exactly one of two
distinct properties of the $\cW(p)$ bar cohomology. The tempering
implication routes through the other property.

\emph{(A) Correlation-function Massey boundedness.} The amplitude norm
\[
 \bigl\| \langle \Omega_{i_1}, \ldots, \Omega_{i_k}\rangle_{\mathrm{corr}}
 \bigr\|
 \;:=\;
 \sup_{z_j}\,
 \bigl\lvert
 \langle \Omega_{i_1}(z_1) \cdots \Omega_{i_k}(z_k)\rangle_{\cW(p)}
 \bigr\rvert
\]
is the natural norm on correlation functions. Gurarie 1993 and
Flohr 1996 construct explicit amplitudes with $(\log z)^k$ growth in
the coincidence limit; this norm exceeds every power of
$\dim A(\cW(p))$.

\emph{(B) Shadow-tower Massey boundedness.} The shadow-tower
coefficient $S_r(\cW(p))$ is the Orlik-Solomon residue of the bar
propagator against the universal Arnold / $\dlog$ form on
$\mathrm{FM}_r(\CC)$. Concretely,
\[
 S_r(\cW(p))
 \;=\;
 \bigl\langle [\Barch(\cW(p))]_r,\;
 \dlog\, E(z_{ij})_{\mathrm{Arnold}}
 \bigr\rangle_{\mathrm{FM}_r(\CC)},
\]
a structure constant of the $A_\infty$ coproduct extracted by the
regularised residue of the bar propagator. The required estimate is
the regular-residue bound of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}: exponential,
not factorial, growth in $r$.

The Gurarie-Flohr amplitudes live in the $(\log z_{ij})^n$-enriched
correlation sector; the shadow tower lives on the regular
Orlik-Solomon residue sector. The projection from logarithmic
amplitudes to regular residues, together with the bound on the
resulting coefficients, is the content of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}.

The split is:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item (a) \emph{Correlation-function Massey boundedness on $\cW(p)$}:
 falsified by Gurarie-Flohr.
\item (b) \emph{Shadow-tower Massey boundedness on $\cW(p)$}:
 exponential bound on $|S_r|$ in $r$, equivalently
 $\limsup (|S_r|/r!)^{1/r} = 0$. Open. The
 Adamovi\'c-Milas character-expansion route of
 Remark~\ref{rem:wp-tempering-scope} addresses (b) directly.
\end{itemize}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp} is claim (b);
Gurarie-Flohr falsifies (a).
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Regular--nilpotent--logarithmic shadow decomposition]
\label{def:wp-reg-nil-log-shadow-decomposition}
Fix $p \ge 2$ and choose configuration-space cutoffs and branch sectors
for the logarithmic boundary fields. The full triplet shadow coefficient
has the Jordan decomposition
\[
 S_r(\cW(p))
 \;=\;
 S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}(\cW(p))
 + S_r^{\mathrm{nil}}(\cW(p))
 + S_r^{\log}(\cW(p)).
\]
The first summand is ordinary semisimple propagation and is refined by
Definition~\ref{def:wp-shadow-channel-decomposition} as
\[
 S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}
 = S_r^{TT}+S_r^{TW}+S_r^{WW}.
\]
The second summand is produced by nilpotent $L_0$-Jordan insertions.
The third summand records explicit powers of
$\log(z_i-z_j)$ in logarithmic boundary-changing correlators. The
$TT/TW/WW$ split is therefore a decomposition of
$S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}$, not of the full logarithmic shadow.
\end{definition}

\begin{conjecture}[Logarithmic boundary-changing amplitude bound;
licensing $\alpha+\gamma+\delta+\varepsilon$;
\ClaimStatusConjectured{}]
\label{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}
Let $\phi_{0,1}$ denote the logarithmic boundary field in the
triplet $\cW(p)$ screening realisation. For every fixed $p \ge 2$,
after fixing configuration-space cutoffs and logarithmic branch sectors,
there exist constants $C,M < \infty$, $\varepsilon>0$, and collision
exponents $N_{ij}$ such that for all arities $r$ the regular
logarithmic-sector correlators satisfy
\[
 \left|
 \left\langle
 \phi_{0,1}(z_1)\cdots \phi_{0,1}(z_r)
 \right\rangle
 \right|
 \;\le\;
 C^r (r!)^{1-\varepsilon}
 \prod_{i<j}|z_i-z_j|^{-N_{ij}}
 \left(1+\sum_{i<j}|\log |z_i-z_j||\right)^M .
\]
Equivalently, after the regularised Orlik--Solomon residue defining the
shadow coefficient,
\[
 \limsup_{r\to\infty}
 \left(\frac{|S_r(\cW(p))|}{r!}\right)^{1/r}=0
\]
is reduced to the regular bound
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound} together with
the logarithmic estimate
\[
 |S_r^{\log}(\cW(p))|
 \;\le\;
 C^r(r!)^{1-\varepsilon}.
\]
Finite-dimensionality of the Zhu algebra and $C_2$-cofiniteness do not
imply this estimate. They control finite graded fibres; the displayed
bound controls arity growth through explicit logarithmic powers.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Triplet frontier after the logarithmic split]
The finite pole-envelope model of
Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-finite-pole-envelope-reduction} and the
regular bound of Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
address $S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}$. They do not control
$S_r^{\log}$. The missing theorem for the logarithmic sector is the
subfactorial bound
$|S_r^{\log}|\le C^r(r!)^{1-\varepsilon}$. Until that estimate and the
regular $TW/WW$ estimate are proved, $\cW(p)$ remains a frontier family,
not a theorem-level member of the tempered stratum.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Tempered stratum contains every logarithmic
$\cW(p)$ triplet]
\label{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}
For every integer $p \ge 2$, the logarithmic triplet algebra
$\cW(p)$ at central charge $c(p) = 1 - 6(p-1)^2 / p$ satisfies the
analytic tempering hypothesis
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}
 \bigl( \lvert S_r(\cW(p)) \rvert / r! \bigr)^{1/r}
 \;=\; 0,
\]
with conjectural ordinary-generating convergence radius
\[
 \rho_*(\cW(p)) > 0.
\]
The exact radius is part of the conjectural amplitude problem: the
formal channel bounds suggest the candidates
$\rho_{TT}=\lvert c(p)\rvert/6$,
$\rho_{TW}=\lvert c(p)\rvert/(12p-6)$, and
$\rho_{WW}=\lvert c(p)\rvert/(4p-3)$, so the full radius is not
determined by the $WW$ channel alone. The conjecture also requires the
logarithmic boundary estimate of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}; finite
Zhu data do not supply it.
If this holds, the KS-$\rho$ Banach model satisfies the tempering input
of Theorem~\ref{thm:chain-level-e3-original-complex-conditional} at
every $\cW(p)$, $p \ge 2$; the chain-level $E_3$-topological structure
on the original curved-Dunn complex additionally requires vanishing of
the Banach transfer class $\mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{Ban}}$ and the
finite-propagation class $\mathfrak{o}_{\oplus}$.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[$\cW(p)$ tempering conjecture]
\label{rem:wp-tempering-scope}
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp} stands as a
conjecture, not a theorem under named hypotheses. The Zhu-bounded-Massey
route to the tempered-stratum claim
(Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-zhu-bounded-masseys},
Remark~\ref{rem:zhu-bounded-massey-retraction}) does not survive
primary-source inspection of the Gurarie-Flohr logarithmic amplitudes
(Remark~\ref{rem:gurarie-flohr-massey-obstruction}):
$C_2$-cofiniteness does not entail bounded Masseys in the relevant
sense.

The three-channel $TT + TW + WW$ decomposition
(Section~\ref{sec:wp-three-channel}) supplies polynomial bounds on the
Virasoro-only $TT$ subchannel
(Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}) unconditionally. The
$TW$ and $WW$ channels remain open; the Adamovi\'c-Milas
$\phi_{0,1}$ character expansion and the logarithmic boundary estimate
of Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}
provide a direct computational route to $S_r(\cW(p))$ amplitudes
bypassing the Zhu-Massey argument. The immediate numerical check is
$\lvert S_r \rvert = O(r^{O(1)})$ versus $O(r!)$ at small $p$
($p = 2, 3, 4, 5$). The precise content needed is
Conjectures~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
and~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}.

The logical structure is the following. From $C_2$-cofiniteness of
$\cW(p)$ ($p \ge 2$) with finite-dimensional Zhu algebra, the
implication chain $C_2$-cofiniteness $\Rightarrow$ bounded Masseys
$\Rightarrow$ tempering breaks at the first arrow: Gurarie-Flohr
amplitudes exhibit unbounded Masseys at finite-dimensional Zhu. The
$\cW(p)$ Virasoro subchannel is tempered unconditionally; the $TW$
and $WW$ channels admit a direct-amplitude bound via Adamovi\'c-Milas
character data. The Gurarie-Flohr falsification targets
correlation-function Massey boundedness in the logarithmic amplitude
sector. The shadow tower requires a regular-residue amplitude bound,
distinct from a Zhu-trace bound on logarithmic correlation amplitudes,
together with the subfactorial logarithmic residue estimate of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}. Thus
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp} is scoped by
Conjectures~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
and~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Implication from channel and logarithmic bounds to full tempering]
Assume the conjectural $TW$ and $WW$ regular-sector bounds of
Conjecture~\ref{prop:wp-channel-upper-bounds} and the logarithmic
bound of

If the conjecture is proved, Theorem~\ref{thm:chain-level-e3-original-complex-conditional}
then supplies the Banach convergence input; raw original-complex

\end{remark}

\section{The non-tempered locus after the triplet reduction}
\label{sec:non-tempered-locus-triplet-reduction}

The logarithmic-triplet reduction, combined with the

governed by Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}:

\begin{corollary}[Dichotomy: non-tempered stratum is empty on
the non-logarithmic $C_2$-cofinite landscape; empty on the full
$C_2$-cofinite landscape under
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}]
\label{cor:wp-dichotomy-healed}
\emph{Scope.} The following two-part statement separates the
unconditional non-logarithmic conclusion from the conditional
logarithmic extension; each part is recorded
individually.

\emph{Part (A). Non-logarithmic $C_2$-cofinite landscape
(unconditional).}
The non-tempered stratum of class $\mathsf{M} \cup \mathsf{M}^*$ on
the \emph{non-logarithmic} $C_2$-cofinite landscape is empty:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Virasoro generic-$c$: tempered
 (Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro}).
\item Principal $\cW_3$ generic-$c$: tempered
 (Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-w3}).
\item Principal $\cW_N$, $N \ge 4$: tempered on the finite-envelope
 locus
 (Theorem~\ref{thm:wn-tempered-all-N} in
 Chapter~\ref{ch:wn-tempered-closure}); Stirling dominance applies at
 every finite $\beta_N$ after the Riccati envelope is supplied. The
 harmonic closed form
 $\beta_N = 12(H_N - 1) = \sum_{s=2}^{N} 12/s$ is
 Conjecture~\ref{conj:beta-N-harmonic-closed-form}, not an
 independent all-rank proof.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Part (B). Logarithmic extension under the triplet conjecture.}
The extension of Part (A) to the full $C_2$-cofinite standard
landscape (adding the logarithmic triplet and singlet) reads:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Logarithmic triplet $\cW(p)$, $p \ge 2$: tempered under
 Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}; this requires
 both the regular semisimple bound of
 Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound} and the
 logarithmic boundary estimate of
 Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}. The
 Gurarie-Flohr amplitudes show that finite Zhu data do not imply the
 logarithmic estimate.
\item Singlet $\cM(p)$, $p \ge 2$: tempered under the direct
 analogue of Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}; the
 singlet has its own logarithmic regular-sector amplitudes and is not
 a Virasoro-only reduction.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Chain-level consequence.} Every VOA in Part (A) satisfies the
tempering input for the Banach model; every VOA in Part (B) satisfies
that input under Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}.
Original-complex $E_3$ descent in both parts is the additional
obstruction condition
$\mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{Ban}}=\mathfrak{o}_{\oplus}=0$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:chain-level-e3-original-complex-conditional}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Part (A) is a direct combination of
Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro},
Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-w3}, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:wn-tempered-all-N}, each cited at its stated
unconditional scope. Part (B) follows by combining Part (A) with
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}; the singlet case
requires the parallel regular and logarithmic amplitude bounds for its
logarithmic generator. No Virasoro-only reduction is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The non-tempered locus after the triplet reduction]
\label{rem:non-tempered-frontier-unbounded-zhu}
After the triplet reduction, the finite-Zhu logarithmic triplet remains
a two-part amplitude problem:
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound} for
$S_r^{\mathrm{reg}}$ and
Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound} for
$S_r^{\log}$. Outside that finite-Zhu problem, the remaining
non-tempered candidates on the class-$\mathsf{M}^*$ locus are VOAs
with infinite-dimensional Zhu algebra.

Three explicit candidates survive as open problems:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Monster-type twisted sectors (Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman)}:
 the full orbifold $\cV^\natural$ of the Leech lattice VOA by the
 $\mathbb{Z} / 2$ Leech involution, viewed as a sum of twisted sectors,
 has a logarithmic pyramid of pseudo-trace modules whose total
 dimension is infinite (Miyamoto 2004). The open question
 is whether the shadow tower of the whole pyramid (as opposed to
 the vacuum sector alone) is tempered.
\item \textbf{Coset realisations at irrational central charge
 (Creutzig-Kanade-Linshaw)}: commutants
 $\mathrm{Com}(H_{\kappa}, \cA)$ for irrational $\kappa$ typically
 have infinite-dim Zhu algebra generated by a non-rational lattice.
 Tempering of such cosets is open.
\item \textbf{Non-rational affine minimal cosets
 (Adamovi\'c et al.)}: $\widehat{\mathfrak{sl}}_2$ at admissible
 levels $k = -2 + p/q$ has a logarithmic module category with
 infinite-dim Zhu algebra. Tempering is open.
\end{enumerate}
These are the infinite-Zhu problems. The triplet $\cW(p)$ is a
separate finite-Zhu problem until
Conjectures~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
and~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound} are proved.
\end{remark}

\begin{conjecture}[Finite-Zhu amplitude criterion for tempering]
\label{conj:tempered-unbounded-zhu}
Let $\cA$ be a simple $C_2$-cofinite chiral algebra in the standard
landscape, chirally Koszul in the Vol~I sense. Suppose that its
bar-graph amplitudes admit a family-specific subfactorial bound: after
splitting into regular, nilpotent, and logarithmic channels, each
summand is bounded either by an exponential-polynomial sequence
$C^r r^d$ or by $C^r(r!)^{1-\varepsilon}$ for some
$\varepsilon>0$. Then $\cA$ is analytically tempered:
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty}
 \bigl( \lvert S_r(\cA) \rvert / r! \bigr)^{1/r}
 \;=\; 0.
\]
Finite-dimensionality of $A(\cA)$ is not itself the bound; it is only
one algebraic input in the channel estimate.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{remark}[Evidence for Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-unbounded-zhu}]
\label{rem:evidence-for-tempering-criterion}
The evidence is family-specific. Generic Virasoro and generic affine
$V_k(\mathfrak g)$ are not $C_2$-cofinite inputs; their tempering comes
from the Virasoro recurrence and the class~$\mathsf L$ finite-depth
bar calculation. Rational Virasoro minimal quotients, positive-definite
lattice VOAs, Schellekens holomorphic rows, and Monster
$V^\natural$ have finite algebraic shadows only together with the
finite-depth, finite-envelope, or finite-orbifold BV data used in their
own proofs. Principal $\cW_N$ is unconditional for $N=3$ and
finite-envelope conditional for $N\ge4$. The logarithmic $\cW(p)$ and
singlet $\cM(p)$ rows remain conjectural: the regular-sector shadow
bound and the logarithmic $\phi_{0,1}$ boundary-amplitude bound are
both pending, and Gurarie--Flohr rules out deriving the latter from
finite Zhu data. Thus Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-unbounded-zhu}
records an amplitude package to be proved, not a Zhu-only criterion.
\end{remark}

\section{Independent derivations}
\label{sec:wp-iv-summary}

The proved theorems and conjectural statements have the following
verification and evidence structure, with disjoint source families for
each statement.

\textbf{1. Virasoro-sub-channel Kac-exclusion
(Lemma~\ref{lem:wp-virasoro-subchannel-tempered}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: direct algebraic solution of
 $c(p) = 1 - 6(p-1)^2/p = -22/5$: no integer $p \ge 2$ solution.
\item \emph{Verified against}: the triplet central-charge formula
 evaluated at $p = 2, 3, 4, 5, 10$ and compared numerically to
 $-22/5 = -4.4$: all values distinct.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: derivation uses algebraic Kac-zero
 solution; verification uses the independent triplet central-charge
 formula.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{2. Channel-radius arithmetic
(Conjecture~\ref{prop:wp-channel-upper-bounds}, outside the proved
$TT$ line).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro}
 for the $TT$ line, plus the Adamovi\'c-Milas OPE pole orders for
 the conjectural $TW$ and $WW$ regular-sector lines.
\item \emph{Verified against}: the Virasoro shadow recurrence
 at $c = c(p)$ for $p = 2, 3, 4, 5$ through $r = 8$; the $TW$ and
 $WW$ entries are radius arithmetic targets, not full-channel
 computations. The arithmetic check records that the formal bottleneck
 is $TW$, not $WW$.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: the proved $TT$ line uses the
 Virasoro recurrence. The $TW$ and $WW$ lines require the logarithmic
 $\cW(p)$ shadow recurrence.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{3. Zhu algebra dimension, not Zhu-bounded Masseys
(Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-zhu-finite}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: Adamovi\'c-Milas 2008
 Proposition~4.3 ($\dim A(\cW(p)) = 2p$).
\item \emph{Verified against}: independent computation of the
 Adamovi\'c-Milas formula for $p = 2, 3, 4, 5$ giving
 $\{4, 6, 8, 10\}$.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: derivation cites the Adamovi\'c-Milas
 paper; verification re-derives the dimension formula arithmetically
 via the triplet's 2 trivial + $p-1$ non-trivial + $p-1$ pseudo-trace
 module count. It does not verify any Massey bound.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{4. Finite free strong generation obstruction
(Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-c2-forbids-finite-free-strong-generation}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: Adamovi\'c--Milas $C_2$-cofiniteness plus
 the Li--PBW description of the $C_2$ quotient of a freely strongly
 generated vertex algebra.
\item \emph{Verified against}: direct Hilbert-series calculation for the
 polynomial $C_2$ quotient
 $\CC[x_T,x_{W^+},x_{W^0},x_{W^-}]$ with degrees
 $(2,2p-1,2p-1,2p-1)$.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: the proof uses the structural
 implication ``free strong generation $\Rightarrow$ polynomial
 $C_2$ quotient''; the Hilbert series detects infinite support
 without using triplet characters.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{5. Regular-sector amplitude bound
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: not proved; this is the exact missing
 lemma for $TW/WW$ triplet tempering.
\item \emph{Finite reduction}: Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-finite-pole-envelope-reduction}
 proves that any finite residue-state pole-envelope realisation of
 the regular sector gives the required exponential-polynomial bound.
 For the $\cW(2)$ model the exact constants are
 $C_2=4$, $N_2=3$, $R_2=240$.
\item \emph{Verified against}: finite-model
 bounds enforce the scope by rejecting finite-free-generation, full
 $\cW(2)$ truncation, and full-triplet radius formulas as proved
 inputs; the factorial-growth probe rejects any sequence with $r!$
 growth.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: the finite-model bound carries only
 the combinatorial reduction; the actual $\cW(p)$ factorisation
 through that model is the open mathematical input.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{6. Logarithmic boundary-changing amplitude bound
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: not proved; this is the exact missing
 logarithmic-sector estimate for the $\phi_{0,1}$ boundary-changing
 correlators after branch and configuration-space cutoffs are fixed.
\item \emph{Finite reduction}: any bound
 $|S_r^{\log}|\le C^r(r!)^{1-\varepsilon}$ gives
 $\limsup(|S_r^{\log}|/r!)^{1/r}=0$ by Stirling, because
 $(C^r/(r!)^\varepsilon)^{1/r}\to0$.
\item \emph{Verified against}: the Gurarie-Flohr logarithmic amplitudes
 force the statement to remain independent of finite Zhu algebra and
 $C_2$-cofiniteness.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: this is a logarithmic boundary-field
 estimate, not a semisimple $TT/TW/WW$ pole-envelope computation.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{7. Universal $\cW(p)$ tempering
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: combination of
 the proved $TT$ line and
 Conjectures~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
 and~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}.
\item \emph{Verified against}: numerical extraction of
 $(\lvert S_r^{TT} \rvert / r!)^{1/r}$ at $r = 8$ from the Vol~I
 Virasoro recurrence at $c = c(p)$ for $p = 3, 4, 5$; values all
 well below $1/e \approx 0.368$.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: this is evidence for the $TT$ line
 only. It does not compute the full triplet shadow tower.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{8. Dichotomy on $C_2$-cofinite
(Corollary~\ref{cor:wp-dichotomy-healed}).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \emph{Derived from}: combination of
 Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-virasoro},
 Theorem~\ref{thm:tempered-stratum-contains-w3}, and
 Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}.
\item \emph{Verified against}: explicit enumeration of
 $C_2$-cofinite families with the logarithmic triplet and singlet
 entries marked conjectural, not verified.
\item \emph{Disjoint rationale}: the corollary is a logical
 combination; the verification enumerates the known
 $C_2$-cofinite families.
\end{itemize}

\section{Logarithmic locus in the tempered stratum}
\label{sec:wp-tempered-stratum-location}

\begin{remark}[Triplet reduction]
\label{rem:wp-triplet-reduction}
The triplet algebra $\cW(p)$ realises class $\mathsf{M}^*$ structure:
non-free strong generation is genuine but does not by itself entail
factorial growth. The two assertions split.
Proposition~\ref{prop:wp-c2-forbids-finite-free-strong-generation}
excludes finite free strong generation for the $C_2$-cofinite triplet;
finite PBW agreement is therefore not a proof route. The
Zhu-bounded-Massey mechanism does not control regular shadow bounds or
the logarithmic boundary-changing estimate. The strongest closed form
is therefore the pair
Conjectures~\ref{conj:wp-regular-sector-amplitude-bound}
and~\ref{conj:wp-logarithmic-boundary-amplitude-bound}; finite-Zhu
tempering takes the amplitude-package form of
Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-unbounded-zhu}, not a Zhu-only
Massey-bound implication.
\end{remark}

The logarithmic $\cW(p)$ problem from
Chapter~\ref{ch:tempered-stratum-characterization} reads as follows.
The algebra $\cW(p)$ was one of three candidates for the non-empty
non-tempered stratum; its $TT$ sub-channel is tempered for every
$p \ge 2$, and the full triplet statement reduces to the regular-sector
$TW/WW$ bound and the logarithmic $\phi_{0,1}$ boundary estimate
inside Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}.
Original-complex
$E_3$ descent imposes the additional vanishing
$\mathfrak{o}_{\mathrm{Ban}}=\mathfrak{o}_{\oplus}=0$. Outside this
problem the open candidates are Monster-type twisted sectors with
infinite pyramid, coset realisations at irrational central charge, and
non-rational affine minimal cosets. Companion sources:
Chapter~\ref{ch:tempered-stratum-characterization} for Virasoro and
$\cW_3$ tempering; the FGST theta representation for the $\cW(p)$
structure; and the Adamovi\'c-Milas generator count and Zhu algebra
dimension.

The universal tempering conjecture
(Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-unbounded-zhu}) becomes a natural
endpoint once Conjecture~\ref{conj:tempered-stratum-contains-wp}
closes. The current dichotomy stands at the weaker form: every
non-logarithmic $C_2$-cofinite family is tempered, and the
logarithmic triplet and singlet sit as regular/logarithmic amplitude
problems.
