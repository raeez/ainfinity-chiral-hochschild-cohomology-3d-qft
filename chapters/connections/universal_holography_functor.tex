\chapter{The Universal Holography Theorem}
\label{ch:universal-holography-functor}

\section*{Prologue}

What is the universal forced holographic extension of an
$\SCchtop$-algebra? By the bicoloured-primitive universality
theorem
\textup(\cref{thm:bicoloured-primitive-universality}\textup) every
$\SCchtop$-algebra is canonically presented by its bicoloured
primitive package
$\primPkgBicolour$
on $(C, \R)$
\textup(\cref{def:bicoloured-primitive-package}\textup); the chart
algebra $\bdyAlg = A_b = \mathrm{End}_{\openFactCat}(b)$ at chosen
boundary vacuum $b$ produces the level-$\mathsf{C}$ projection.
Adding a conformal vector $\omega \in A_b$ at non-critical level
and an HT realization datum
\(\Xi(A_b,\omega,\mathrm{BL})\) gives the holographic extension:
the datum supplies a holomorphic-topological factorisation algebra
on $X \times \R$, a boundary comparison with $\bdyAlg$, and a
bulk--Hochschild comparison from the level-$\mathsf{Z}$ derived
chiral centre
$\bulkOf{A_b} = \Zder(\bdyAlg) = \bulkChirHoch{A_b}$. The
construction is the $\Xi$-decorated functor
\[
 \Phihol
 \;\colon\;
 \ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X
 \longrightarrow
 \HTQFT^{\Xi}_{X \times \R},
\]
from the admissible boundary-linear category of chiral algebras with
conformal vector and realization datum on a smooth projective curve
$X$ to the category of holomorphic-topological gauge theories on
$X \times \R$ with specified comparison maps. By
\cref{cor:universal-holography-via-bicoloured-universality}, $\Phihol$
factors as
$\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}_{\mathrm{bic}} \circ \mathsf{Prim}^{\mathrm{bic}}$:
Universal Holography is the universal stage chain made physical only
after the physical realization datum has been supplied. The boundary
observables of $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)$
are identified with~$\cA$ by
\(\eta^\partial_\cA\); the bulk observables are identified with
\(\Zder(\cA)\) by
\(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\) in the ambient in which the corresponding
chain complex converges. Classes G, L, and C live on the ordinary
chain complex. Class~M lives on the weight-completed pro-ambient; its
underlying $E_2$-chiral / $\SCchtop$ comparison is chain-level under
the declared hypotheses, while the strict $E_3$ chain lift is taken
under the explicit hypotheses stated in the chiral Higher Deligne
chapter. The exact-sector Virasoro HT model underlying the proposed
3d quantum gravity discussion is the value of this functor at the
Brown--Henneaux Virasoro datum
\(\Xi_{\Vir_c}^{\mathrm{BH}}\). Monster moonshine is the value at the
Leech orbifold only after the finite-orbifold BV realization datum is
supplied.

The class-M input is the DS-Hochschild compatibility bridge of
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}
(Chapter~\ref*{ch:chiral-higher-deligne}). Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction
transports class~M to class~L on the weight-completed complex, and
class~L is the Costello-Li / Costello-Gaiotto holomorphic
Chern-Simons sector. The bridge says that chiral Hochschild cochains
commute with Drinfeld-Sokolov reduction; the functor says that the
bulk-boundary HT theory commutes with the same reduction. These are
the same compatibility seen from the algebra and field-theory sides.

Four theorem clusters are separated by this single functor:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em]
\item \emph{Bulk projection, global triangle, and chiral HKR:} the
 scalar $\C[\![c]\!]$ quotient, the boundary-linear triangle, and
 the HKR comparison are saddle-point or cohomological shadows of the
 chain-level functorial statement.
\item \emph{Perturbative finiteness.} The functor $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$
 separates algebraic from physical finiteness: the former is
 bar-complex, the latter is Costello RG; the bridge to physical UV is
 weight-completed for class~M and strict $E_3$ chain-level only under
 the chiral Higher Deligne lift.
\item \emph{Monster orbifold.} The Leech lattice route is a
finite-orbifold criterion: the obstruction is the class
$[\alpha_{\mathrm{orb}}]\in H^3(B\Z/2;\mathrm{U}(1))$, and the
functor extends to the orbifold after the local BV datum
$\alpha_{\mathrm{orb}}(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)=+1$ and
finite-orbifold BV descent have been supplied.
\item \emph{Shadow, holographic, and universal-IS statements.} These
 split into three clean statements, with class-M chain-level claims
 read in the weight-completed ambient.
\end{itemize}

The chapter therefore has a fixed form: define the source and target
categories, construct $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ on the $\Xi$-decorated
source, prove the class-M compatibility in the correct ambient, and
then specialise the functor to Virasoro, the Monster, the
perturbative-finiteness split, and the boundary-linear reconstruction
statements.

\section{The two categories}
\label{sec:uhf-setup}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\subsection{Source: boundary-linear chiral algebras with conformal vector}

Fix a smooth projective complex curve $X$.

\begin{definition}[Boundary-linear chiral algebra with conformal
vector]
\label{def:uhf-chiralg-omega-bl}
A \emph{boundary-linear chiral algebra with conformal vector on
$X$} is a tuple $(\cA, \omega, T_\omega)$ where:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item $\cA$ is an $E_1$-chiral algebra on $X$ (in the sense of
 Beilinson-Drinfeld, equivalently a $\ChirAss$-algebra) carrying a
 $\D_X$-module structure;
\item $\omega \in \cA_2(X)$ is a \emph{conformal vector}: a
 weight-$2$ element whose OPE with itself reproduces the Virasoro
 OPE with central charge $c(\cA) \in \C$, and such that
 $L_{-1} := \mathrm{Res}_z z^0 \omega(z)$ generates $\D_X$-module
 translations;
\item the level parameter $k(\cA)$ lies off the critical locus
 (equivalently, the Sugawara inner stress tensor $\SugA$ is
 defined: for affine $\hat\fg$, $k \neq -h^\vee$; for
 Virasoro/$\cW_N$, $c$ generic; for Heisenberg / lattice, trivially
 non-critical);
\item $\cA$ is \emph{boundary-linear} in the sense of
 Vol~II~Ch.~\ref*{chap:ht-bulk-boundary-line}: the shadow obstruction
 tower $\Theta_\cA$ is a Maurer-Cartan element of
 $\mathfrak{g}^{\mathrm{mod}}_\cA$ \textup{and} the associated
 twisting bar-cobar square admits a strict splitting on the
 boundary face (equivalently, the Lagrangian complementarity
 datum of Theorem~C carries a strict fibre-center eigendecomposition
 at chain level on the $\cA$-face of the Koszul pair).
\end{enumerate}
A \emph{morphism} $f \colon (\cA, \omega_\cA, T_\cA) \to
(\cB, \omega_\cB, T_\cB)$ is a chiral-algebra morphism of
underlying $E_1$-chiral algebras compatible with conformal vectors:
$f(\omega_\cA) = \omega_\cB$ on the nose if $c(\cA) = c(\cB)$, and
more generally $f(\omega_\cA) - \omega_\cB$ is an
$L_{-1}$-descendant of lower conformal weight
(DS-compatible conformal shift). The resulting category is
$\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL}}_X$. Its full subcategory
$\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm}}_X$ consists of those objects for
which the relevant generic or admissible Kazhdan--Lusztig / periodic-CDG
exactness hypotheses used below are verified.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Admissible holographic input]
\label{def:uhf-xi-input}
An \emph{admissible holographic input} is a tuple
\[
(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)
\]
where $(\cA,\omega,T_\omega)$ lies in
$\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm}}_X$ and
\(\Xi_\cA\) consists of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item a Costello--Gwilliam HT BV/factorization model
 $\cF_{\cA,\Xi}$ on $X\times\R$ with the chosen boundary condition;
\item a boundary comparison quasi-isomorphism
 \[
 \eta^\partial_\cA\colon
 \Obs^\partial(\cF_{\cA,\Xi})\xrightarrow{\simeq}\cA
 \]
 of $E_1$-chiral factorisation algebras on $X$;
\item a bulk--Hochschild comparison map in the declared ambient
 \[
 \chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\colon
 \Zder(\cA)\longrightarrow
 \Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF_{\cA,\Xi});
 \]
\item the ambient category in which the displayed maps are asserted:
 the ordinary chain complex for classes G/L/C and the
 weight-completed/pro ambient for class~M, including
 \(\hypAmbientWtCpl\), DS--Hochschild transport, cover descent for
 fractional good gradings when present, and the two-coloured cobar
 homotopy whenever a strict mixed chain-level refinement is claimed.
\end{enumerate}
Morphisms of admissible holographic inputs are conformal-vector
preserving chiral algebra morphisms together with compatible maps of
the HT models and the two comparison maps. The resulting category is
\(\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\). Thus
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}\) below is not a bare functor from boundary
algebras alone.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Three worlds of chiral Hochschild]
\label{rem:uhf-three-hochschilds}
In the sequel $\ChirHoch^\bullet$ is always the \emph{chiral}
Hochschild of the $\cW$ on $X$
(Vol~II Definition~\ref*{def:chiral_hochschild}). It is not the
topological $\HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{mode}}$ of the mode algebra, nor
the Gel'fand-Fuchs cohomology $\HH^\bullet_{\mathrm{GF}}$ of
formal vector fields. These three live in different operadic
contexts (chiral $E_2$ on a curve / topological $E_2$ over $\Z$ /
Lie cohomology on the formal disc) and agree only after several
chain-level identifications, each disclosed at the point of use.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Target: HT-QFT on $X \times \R$}

\begin{definition}[HT-QFT on $X \times \R$]
\label{def:uhf-ht-qft-factorization}
A \emph{holomorphic-topological quantum field theory on
$X \times \R$} is a Costello-Gwilliam factorization algebra
$\cF$ on the space $X \times \R$ together with a decomposition of
its structure into:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item a \emph{holomorphic direction} $X$, on which $\cF$ is
 holomorphic (sections depend holomorphically on
 $\bar\partial_X$-data; the $\bar\partial_X$-cohomology gives the
 chiral content);
\item a \emph{topological direction} $\R_{\ge0}$ \textup{(}or a slab
 $[0,1]$\textup{)}, on which $\cF$ is
 locally constant (Dunn-additive along $\R$, so that the
 $\R$-direction supplies one $E_1^{\mathrm{top}}$-factor of the
 bulk local operators algebra);
\item a \emph{boundary datum}: on $X\times\{0\}$ \textup{(}or on both
 slab faces\textup{)} one has a boundary factorization algebra on~$X$,
 related to the bulk by a chain-level restriction morphism.
\end{enumerate}
A \emph{morphism} of HT-QFTs is a map of factorization algebras on
$X \times \R$ preserving the holomorphic-topological split and the
boundary data. The category $\HTQFT_{X \times \R}$ is the
$\infty$-category of such objects.

The $\Xi$-decorated target
\(\HTQFT^\Xi_{X \times \R}\) is the category whose objects are
HT-QFTs together with the boundary comparison
\(\eta^\partial\), the bulk--Hochschild comparison
\(\chi_{\mathrm{HT}}\), and the ambient declaration carried by
\(\Xi\); morphisms preserve these data.

For $\cF \in \HTQFT_{X \times \R}$ we write $\Obs^\partial(\cF)$ for
the boundary factorization algebra (chiral on $X$) and
$\Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF)$ for the bulk factorization algebra
(HT on $X \times \R$). Before topologisation the local operator
object is $\SCchtop$-valued: the $X$-direction is holomorphic and
the $\R$-direction is topological. After a BRST topologisation of
the holomorphic translations has been chosen, Dunn additivity applies
to the resulting topological factors and gives an
$E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$ algebra.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[$E_3$-topological, not $E_3$-chiral on the bulk]
\label{rem:uhf-e3-scope}
The bulk algebra is $E_3$-\emph{topological} after one has
applied the Sugawara topologization of Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower}
(conformal vector $\omega$ makes $L_{-1}$ BRST-exact; the chiral
direction $X$ collapses at cohomology to a topological one
augmenting $\R$). Before topologization the bulk is
$\SCchtop$-valued; after topologization it is
$E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$-valued. The Costello-Gwilliam factorization
algebra $\Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF)$ lives natively on $X \times \R$
and registers both regimes; the $E_3$-topological structure
emerges on the Sugawara-topologized complex (for class~M, in the
weight-completed category, by Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower}
plus the DS-Hochschild bridge
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Why $X \times \R$, not $X \times \R^2$]
\label{rem:uhf-geometry-one-real-direction}
The HT-QFT is 3-real-dimensional ($X$ complex + $\R$ real), the
correct dimension for a 3d gauge theory whose boundary is a 2d CFT.
The Costello-Gaiotto holomorphic Chern-Simons theory lives on
$X \times \R$ for abelian and for the non-abelian theories that
produce the class~M and $\cW$-boundaries (Costello-Gaiotto 2018,
arXiv:1804.06460; 2021, arXiv:2103.01042). Higher-spin gravity in
the class-$\cW_\infty$ limit (Vol~II~\ref*{ch:thqg-gravitational-complexity})
requires a higher-dimensional ambient, outside the scope of the
present functor.
\end{remark}

\section{The functor $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$}
\label{sec:uhf-functor}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{theorem}[Universal holography functor on the
\(\Xi\)-decorated source; \ClaimStatusProvedHere on supplied
realization data; \ClaimStatusConditional on existence of
\(\Xi\); licensing tags \(\alpha+\beta+\gamma+\delta\)]
\label{thm:universal-holography-functor}
On admissible holographic inputs satisfying the physics-bridge
hypotheses, factorized parametrix, one-loop finiteness,
bulk--Hochschild comparison, and boundary-linear exactness
assumptions, there exists a functor
\[
 \Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}
 \;\colon\;
 \ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X
 \longrightarrow
 \HTQFT^{\Xi}_{X \times \R}
\]
characterised by the following four properties. The functor is not
defined from \(\cA\) alone: an object is
\((\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\), and the physical HT model,
boundary comparison, bulk--Hochschild comparison, and ambient are
part of \(\Xi_\cA\). For classes G, L, and C the chain-level
statements are taken on the ordinary chain complex. For class~M they
are taken in the weight-completed / pro ambient; the underlying
$E_2$-chiral and $\SCchtop$ identifications are
\ClaimStatusProvedHere on the supplied comparison surface, while the
strict class-M $E_3$-topological chain lift is the
hypothesis-bound lift of
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chiral-higher-deligne}(2).
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item \textbf{Boundary restriction.} For every
 \((\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA) \in
 \ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\) the factorization algebra
 \(\cF_{\cA,\Xi}:=\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\)
 satisfies
 \[
 \eta^\partial_\cA\colon
 \Obs^\partial(\cF_{\cA,\Xi}) \;\xrightarrow{\simeq}\; \cA
 \]
 as $E_1$-chiral algebras on $X$.
\item \textbf{Bulk identification under the exact comparison.}
 \[
 \chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\colon
 \Zder(\cA) \;\xrightarrow{\simeq}\;
 \Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF_{\cA,\Xi})
 \]
 is the bulk--Hochschild equivalence in the declared ambient on the
 scoped exact-sector comparison surface. After the Sugawara
 topologization of Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower}, this is
	 an $E_3$-topological identification on the ordinary complex for
	 classes G/L/C, and on the class-M weight-completed ambient; the
	 strict class-M chain-level $E_3$ lift is included under the
	 hypotheses stated above.
\item \textbf{DS-functoriality.} For every good $\Z$-grading
 $\Gamma$ on a simple Lie algebra $\fg$ with nilpotent $f$ of
 principal or hook-type, and for \(\Xi\)-data commuting with
 Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction, the square
 \[
 \begin{array}{c}
 \ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X(V_k(\fg))
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\DS_f\;}\;
 \ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X(\cW_k(\fg, f))
 \\
 {\scriptstyle \Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}} \downarrow \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad
 \downarrow {\scriptstyle \Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}}
 \\
 \HTQFT^\Xi_{X \times \R}(T_{V_k(\fg),\Xi})
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\DS_f^{\mathrm{bulk}}\;}\;
 \HTQFT^\Xi_{X \times \R}(T_{\cW_k(\fg,f),\Xi})
 \end{array}
 \]
 commutes up to a natural isomorphism of functors, compatible with
 the DS-Hochschild bridge of Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}.
\item \textbf{Class coverage (Koszul locus, generic level).}
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ is defined chain-level over all four shadow
 classes when the corresponding \(\Xi\)-data are supplied:
 \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
 \item Class~G (Heisenberg / lattice): Costello-Li abelian
 holomorphic Chern-Simons with abelian boundary and its
 bulk--Hochschild comparison, Sugawara tower immediate;
 \item Class~L (affine $\hat\fg$, $k \neq -h^\vee$):
 Costello-Gaiotto holomorphic Chern-Simons on $X \times \R$ with
 gauge group~$G$, boundary comparison, and affine
 bulk--Hochschild comparison; Sugawara at non-critical level;
 \item Class~C ($\beta\gamma$, $bc$): Friedan-Martinec-Shenker
 bosonisation reduces the comparison datum to class~G; Sugawara
 inherited;
 \item Class~M (Virasoro, $\cW_N$, hook-type $\cW_k(\fg, f)$):
 Costello-Gaiotto holomorphic Chern-Simons with
 Drinfeld-Sokolov boundary condition; class~M bulk identification
 in the weight-completed ambient via
 \(\hypAmbientWtCpl\), the DS-Hochschild bridge
 (Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}), and the HT
 bulk--Hochschild comparison map. On the bounded direct-sum complex
 the class-M chain-level $E_3$ identification is false by the MC5
 obstruction; it is not part of this theorem.
 \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Four structural moves: construction, boundary face, bulk face,
class-M ambient.

\emph{Move 1: Construction of $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ on the
\(\Xi\)-source.}
Start with
\((\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\in
\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\). By
Definition~\ref{def:uhf-xi-input}, \(\Xi_\cA\) contains a
Costello--Gwilliam HT BV/factorization model
\(\cF_{\cA,\Xi}\) on \(X\times\R\), a boundary comparison
\(\eta^\partial_\cA\), a bulk--Hochschild comparison
\(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\), and the ambient declaration. Define
\[
 \Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)
 := \cF_{\cA,\Xi}.
\]
A morphism in
\(\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\) is, by definition, a
chiral-algebra morphism together with a compatible map of the HT
models and of the two comparison maps; applying the HT-model map
defines \(\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}\) on morphisms. Composition and units are
inherited from the \(\infty\)-category of HT factorization algebras.

The Costello-Gwilliam factorisation envelope of~\(\cA\) along the
holomorphic direction \(X\) remains load-bearing: it supplies the
holomorphic boundary face (Costello-Gwilliam Vol.~II, Theorem~3.6.1:
vertex algebras give rise to holomorphic factorisation algebras).
The Hochschild cochain factorisation algebra supplies the algebraic
candidate
\(\ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA,\cA)=\Zder(\cA)\). These two algebraic
constructions do not, by themselves, construct the physical
three-dimensional HT bulk from \(\cA\) alone; the physical
identification is exactly the datum
\(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\).

\emph{Move 2: Boundary face.}
The boundary restriction of \(\cF_{\cA,\Xi}\) is identified with
\(\cA\) by the supplied quasi-isomorphism
\[
 \eta^\partial_\cA\colon
 \Obs^\partial(\cF_{\cA,\Xi})\xrightarrow{\simeq}\cA .
\]
This is a statement in \(E_1\)-chiral factorisation algebras on \(X\).
The Costello-Gwilliam vertex-algebra / factorisation-algebra
equivalence verifies the algebraic boundary model; \(\eta^\partial\)
records its embedding as the chosen boundary face of the HT theory.

\emph{Move 3: Bulk face.}
The chiral Hochschild complex computes the derived chiral centre
\[
 \ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA, \cA)
 \;=\;
 \Zder(\cA).
\]
The statement that this algebraic centre is the local bulk observables
of the chosen HT theory is the comparison map
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cA}\colon
 \Zder(\cA)\xrightarrow{\simeq}
 \Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF_{\cA,\Xi})
\]
in the ambient carried by \(\Xi_\cA\). After Sugawara
topologization (Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower}), Dunn
additivity applies to the topologized factors and gives the
$E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$ structure. For classes G, L, C this is on the
direct-sum complex; for class~M it is a weight-completed statement,
and the algebraic class-M input is Move~4.

\emph{Move 4: Class-M ambient via DS-Hochschild.}
For class~M, Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild} provides the
algebraic Hochschild comparison in the weight-completed ambient:
\[
 \ChirHoch^\bullet(\cW_k(\fg, f))
 \;\xrightarrow{\;\sim\;}\;
 H^\bullet_{\DS}\bigl(\ChirHoch^\bullet(V_k(\fg))\bigr)
\]
as $E_2$-chiral Gerstenhaber algebras (lifted to $E_3$ via
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chiral-higher-deligne}, under that theorem's
explicit chain-level hypotheses for the strict $E_3$ lift). This
controls the derived-centre side. The HT-bulk side is compared to it
only through \(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},\cW}\) in the class-M realization
datum. If the class-L datum \(\Xi_{V_k(\fg)}\) and the class-M datum
\(\Xi_{\cW_k(\fg,f)}\) commute with Drinfeld--Sokolov reduction, then
the DS-Hochschild bridge identifies the algebraic sides and the
compatibility of the two \(\chi_{\mathrm{HT}}\)-maps identifies the HT
bulk sides. This proves DS-functoriality on the supplied
\(\Xi\)-surface. It does not assert a direct-sum class-M equality; the
bounded direct-sum obstruction remains outside the theorem.

Class coverage in (iv) is the list of available realization data:
Costello-Li for class~G, Costello-Gaiotto for class~L, FMS
bosonisation reducing class~C to class~G, and the
\(\hypAmbientWtCpl\) DS-Hochschild plus HT comparison package for
class~M.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope discipline]
\label{rem:uhf-scope}
The functor is defined on
$\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X$: the \emph{conformal
vector}, \emph{boundary-linear} hypothesis, and \emph{HT realization
datum} are essential, and the admissible/generic exactness hypotheses
supply the Koszul comparison surface. Without conformal vector the bulk stays
$\SCchtop$-topological (not $E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$; the topologization
condition fails outside the Koszul locus). Without boundary-linearity
the global triangle bulk $\to$ boundary $\to$ lines $\to$ bulk is not
closed at chain level in this non-boundary-linear regime. Without
\(\Xi\) this chapter has only the algebraic centre candidate
\(\Zder(\cA)\), not a constructed physical HT bulk. At the critical
level $k = -h^\vee$ the coefficient in the generic Sugawara tensor has
a pole, and no Sugawara conformal vector is defined on
$V_{-h^\vee}(\fg)$; this is a genuine restriction of the theorem.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Associator disclosure]
\label{rem:uhf-associator}
The chain-level
$E_3$-identification in the bulk is \emph{associator-dependent}:
different Drinfeld associators $\Phi \in \mathrm{GRT}_1(\Q)$
produce strictifications that are chain-equivalent but not
strictly equal. Cohomologically (e.g.\ at the level of~$\kappa$
and the shadow invariants) the functor is associator-free. For

\section{Monster finite-orbifold BV datum}
\label{sec:uhf-monster}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The Moonshine module $V^\natural$, in the orbifold realisation of
Frenkel-Lepowsky-Meurman, is the $\Z/2$-orbifold of the Leech
lattice VOA $V_\Lambda$. The argument uses the class-G Leech parent
and finite-orbifold descent, not a generic class~M original-complex
lift. The universal holography functor extends to the orbifold only if
the $\Z/2$ orbifold BV anomaly is trivialized. For the Leech
$(-1)$-orbifold the determinant contribution is positive; the
Dijkgraaf-Witten class itself is still the local BV datum.

\begin{theorem}[Monster orbifold BV descent criterion;
\ClaimStatusConditional]
\label{thm:uhf-monster-orbifold-bv-anomaly-vanishes}
Let $V_\Lambda$ be the Leech-lattice vertex operator algebra and
$\sigma \colon V_\Lambda \to V_\Lambda$ the lift of the $-1$
isometry of~$\Lambda$ to $V_\Lambda$. Suppose the Dijkgraaf-Witten BV
anomaly class
\[
 \alpha_{\mathrm{orb}}
 \;\in\;
 H^3(B\Z/2; \mathrm{U}(1))
\]
of the $\Z/2$-orbifold $V_\Lambda / \langle \sigma \rangle$ is
trivialized, equivalently
$\alpha_{\mathrm{orb}}(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)=+1$ for a normalized
multiplicative representative, and suppose finite-orbifold BV descent
applies to the Leech HT model. Then the orbifold chiral algebra
$V^\natural = (V_\Lambda / \langle \sigma \rangle)^+$ lies in the
conditional image of $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ after finite-orbifold
descent supplies a realization datum \(\Xi_{V^\natural}^{\mathrm{orb}}\);
under these hypotheses
\[
\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(V^\natural,\omega_{V^\natural},
T_{V^\natural},\Xi_{V^\natural}^{\mathrm{orb}})
\]
is a well-defined HT-QFT on $X \times \R$ for every curve $X$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The finite-group obstruction lies in
$H^3(B\Z/2;\mathrm{U}(1))\cong\Z/2$.  Let
$\sigma$ denote the non-trivial element.  A normalized multiplicative
$3$-cochain has value~$1$ whenever one argument is the identity, so it
is determined by the sign
$\alpha(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)\in\{\pm1\}$. The cocycle equation on
$(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)$ gives
\[
 \alpha(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)^2=1.
\]
If $\beta$ is a normalized $2$-cochain, then
\[
 (\delta\beta)(\sigma,\sigma,\sigma)
 =
 \beta(\sigma,\sigma)\,
 \beta(1,\sigma)^{-1}\,
 \beta(\sigma,1)\,
 \beta(\sigma,\sigma)^{-1}
 =1.
\]
Thus normalized coboundaries do not change the triple sign; the sign
$+1$ is the trivial Dijkgraaf-Witten class and the sign $-1$ is the
generator.

For the Leech involution, the determinant factor
$\det(1-\sigma|_\Lambda)=2^{24}$ contributes a positive real sign.
This is only a compatibility check on a local anomaly calculation. It
does not decide between the two normalized $3$-cocycle classes above.
The theorem therefore assumes the local BV comparison supplies the
trivial sign. Under that hypothesis, finite-orbifold BV descent
transports the Leech HT factorisation algebra through the orbifold,
and FLM boundary restriction identifies the boundary chiral algebra as
$V^\natural$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Monster as a conditional functor value]
\label{rem:uhf-monster-functor-value}
Theorem~\ref{thm:uhf-monster-orbifold-bv-anomaly-vanishes} places
the Monster inside the functor only under the special Leech $\Z/2$
finite-orbifold descent hypotheses. It is reached from the class-G
Leech lattice model, not from a generic class-$M$ original-complex
upgrade. The Monster CFT is in the conditional image of
$\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ after the local BV anomaly datum,
finite-orbifold BV descent, and the resulting
\(\Xi_{V^\natural}^{\mathrm{orb}}\) comparison datum are supplied.
\end{remark}

\section{Algebraic vs physical perturbative finiteness}
\label{sec:uhf-perturbative-finiteness}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Two notions of ``perturbative finiteness of twisted gravity''
coexist: algebraic bar-complex finiteness and physical
Costello--RG UV finiteness. They are distinct, separated by
$\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$.

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic / physical UV finiteness split;
\ClaimStatusProvedHere]
\label{thm:uhf-perturbative-finiteness-split}
Let
\((\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\in
\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\), and suppose that
\(\cA\) is modular Koszul
(equivalently: $\Theta_\cA \in \MC(\mathfrak{g}^{\mathrm{mod}}_\cA)$
exists and the shadow obstruction tower is Mittag-Leffler; cf.\
Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:mc2-bar-intrinsic}). Then
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\) satisfies two
distinct finiteness properties:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\Roman*)}]
\item \textbf{Algebraic shadow-tower finiteness.} For every
 genus~$g$ and every $n$, the shadow amplitude
 $\int_{\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}} \mathrm{Sh}_{g,n}(\Theta_\cA)$
 is finite. \textup{(}This is the content of the bar-complex /
 $\MC$ side and follows from
 Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:mc2-bar-intrinsic} plus the Fulton-MacPherson
 regulator. Unconditional.\textup{)}
\item \textbf{Physical Costello RG UV finiteness.} For chosen HT
 prefactorization realizations with factorized parametrix and
 one-loop finiteness, the factorisation
 algebra $\Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF_{\cA,\Xi})$ admits a Costello-RG
	 renormalisation group flow with a finite UV limit at chain
	 level on classes G/L/C and in the weight-completed HT ambient for
	 class~M. \textup{(}The strict class-M $E_3$-topological chain model
	 is included under the hypotheses of
	 Theorem~\ref*{thm:chiral-higher-deligne}(2).\textup{)}
\end{enumerate}
(I) is the bar-complex combinatorial statement of
Vol~II~\ref*{thm:thqg-I-perturbative-finiteness};
(II) is direct on classes G/L/C and weight-completed on class~M via
the DS-Hochschild bridge.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\emph{(I): Algebraic finiteness.} The MC element $\Theta_\cA$ is
bar-intrinsic (Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:mc2-bar-intrinsic}); its shadow
amplitudes $\mathrm{Sh}_{g,n}(\Theta_\cA)$ are cohomology classes
on the compact orbifold $\overline{\mathcal{M}}_{g,n}$, and
integration of such classes is bounded. The Fulton-MacPherson
compactification of the configuration space serves as a geometric
regulator; no analytic renormalisation is needed.

\emph{(II): Physical UV finiteness.} The Costello definition of
RG flow on a factorisation algebra requires: (a) the operator
product expansion extends to a global $E_3$-topological structure
at chain level; (b) the extended structure admits
renormalisation-group limits controlled by heat-kernel
regularisation. (a) for class~M on the direct-sum complex is
\emph{false} (genuine MC5 failure at class~M, Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:completed-bar-cobar-strong}).
On the weight-completed complex, (a) is supplied by
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}: the DS-Hochschild bridge
produces a chain-level $E_2$-chiral identification of
$\Zder(\cW_k(\fg, f))$ with the DS cohomology of
$\Zder(V_k(\fg))$. The chosen \(\Xi\)-datum supplies the comparison
from this algebraic centre to the HT bulk factorisation algebra,
which is Costello-Renormalisable by the Costello-Li /
Costello-Gaiotto model. The strict class-M $E_3$ chain enhancement is
the hypothesis-bound lift recorded in
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-functor}; the cohomological
$E_3$ statement is unconditional. (b) is the standard Costello
machinery: RG flow descends from class~L to class~M via the DS retract,
with heat-kernel regularisation compatible with the weight-completed
filtration. The UV limit is defined as the inverse limit over weight
stages, which exists by Milnor / Mittag-Leffler on the
Mittag-Leffler shadow tower.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the split matters]
\label{rem:uhf-finiteness-separation}
The phrase ``perturbative finiteness'' hides two different
mathematical claims. Separating it into algebraic~(I) and physical~(II)
surfaces the content: algebraic finiteness is easy
(bar-complex combinatorics, no RG needed); physical finiteness is
hard (Costello RG, chain-level $E_3$ needed); the DS-Hochschild
bridge supplies the class-M weight-completed ambient.
\end{remark}

Chern--Simons levels, a modular invariant pairing of the two chiral
blocks, an integration cycle in the space of real metrics or flat
connections, and a saddle prescription.  The functorial statement
above is only the chiral Brown--Henneaux exact sector.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-functor} to the
quadruple
\((\mathrm{Vir}_c,\omega_{\Vir},T_{\Vir},
\Xi_{\Vir_c}^{\mathrm{BH}})\) on the Koszul locus (generic \(c\)).
Boundary-linearity holds because Virasoro is class~M with the
weight-completed boundary-linear datum supplied by
Theorem~\ref*{thm:chd-ds-hochschild}
(Virasoro \(=\cW_1(\mathrm{sl}_2,f_{\mathrm{prin}})\), principal).
The resulting HT-QFT has boundary comparison
\[
\eta^\partial_{\Vir_c}\colon
\Obs^\partial(\cF_{\Vir_c,\Xi_{\mathrm{BH}}})
\xrightarrow{\simeq}\mathrm{Vir}_c
\]
and bulk comparison
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{HT},\Vir_c}\colon
\Zder(\mathrm{Vir}_c)\xrightarrow{\simeq}
\Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}(\cF_{\Vir_c,\Xi_{\mathrm{BH}}})
\]
in the class-M weight-completed ambient. The boundary is the 2d
Virasoro CFT at central charge \(c\); the connective classical shadow
of the derived centre is \(\C[\![c]\!]\), the cosmological-constant
parameter. Line operators come from the Koszul dual
\(\mathrm{Vir}_{26-c}\)-module category (abstractly, via the
line-functor of Theorem~\ref*{thm:lines_as_modules}). Under the
Brown--Henneaux dictionary \(c = 3\ell/(2G_N)\), the supplied
\(\Xi_{\Vir_c}^{\mathrm{BH}}\), and the physical modular/saddle
hypotheses, this is the exact-sector Virasoro model proposed for the
boundary-CFT reading of pure 3d gravity on AdS$_3$.  Passing from this
chiral sector to the full non-chiral path integral is a separate
physical operation: one must supply the anti-holomorphic Virasoro
sector, the real-form condition, a modular invariant, an integration
cycle, and a saddle prescription.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Functorial form of the gravity theorem]
\label{rem:uhf-gravity-functorial-form}
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-functor} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:uhf-universal-holography-is-a-theorem} give
universal functoriality on the source category: every admissible
boundary algebra equipped with a realization datum in
$\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X$ has the HT value
\(\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}(\cA,\omega,T_\omega,\Xi_\cA)\) constructed
above. This is a forward construction on the \(\Xi\)-decorated
source, not essential surjectivity onto all boundary-linear HT
targets and not a bare construction from \(\cA\) alone. Virasoro with
\(\Xi_{\Vir_c}^{\mathrm{BH}}\) gives the exact-sector model
underlying the proposed 3d-gravity interpretation.
The same machinery identifies
Monster moonshine (Leech orbifold), higher-spin gravity
($\cW_N$), gauge-theoretic matter ($V_k(\fg)$), and abelian
lattice conformal theories.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Boundary faces of the functor]
\label{rem:uhf-open}
Two boundary faces lie outside the theorem.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.3em]
\item \textbf{Non-boundary-linear sector.} The functor
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ is defined on
 $\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X$.
 Extending to chiral algebras without the boundary-linear
 hypothesis requires the non-perfectness gap of Vol~I

\item \textbf{Off-locus chain-level.} Off the Koszul locus the

 machinery of Theorem~\ref{thm:uhf-perturbative-finiteness-split}.
\end{itemize}
Neither contradicts the central statement.
\end{remark}

\section{Boundary of the theorem}
\label{sec:uhf-not-proved}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

The theorem has the following boundary.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Non-boundary-linear sector.}
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$ is defined on
 $\ChirAlg^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X$. The non-boundary-linear
	 extension remains open.
\item \emph{No abstract bulk/Hochschild theorem for arbitrary
 logarithmic $\SCchtop$.}
 Without a chosen HT prefactorization realization satisfying the
 factorized-parametrix, compact-generation, and exact-sector
 comparison hypotheses, the abstract identification
 $\Obs^{\mathrm{bulk}}\simeq\ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA,\cA)$ is not
 proved here.
\item \emph{Critical level.} $V_{-h^\vee}(\fg)$ is not in the
 source of $\Phi_{\mathrm{hol}}$. The critical-level Sugawara
 collapse gives $E_2^{\mathrm{top}}$ rather than
 $E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$
 \textup{(}Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower}%
 \textup{(5)}\textup{)}.
\item \emph{Non-principal / non-hook nilpotents.} The
 DS-Hochschild bridge
 \textup{(}Proposition~\ref{prop:uhf-ds-hochschild-bridge}%
 \textup{)} covers principal and hook-type nilpotents; subregular
 and minimal reduce to class-C via coset
 \textup{(}Vol~I~\ref*{V1-prop:nonprincipal-coset}%
 \textup{)} with auxiliary free factor; exotic nilpotents are
 open.
\item \emph{Off-locus chain-level.} Off the Koszul locus the
 bulk is weight-completed; direct-sum chain-level fails in
 class~M by a genuine MC5 obstruction
 \textup{(}Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:completed-bar-cobar-strong}%
 \textup{)}.
\item \emph{Higher-spin tower beyond $\cW_\infty$ / $E_3$.}
 Part~\ref*{part:gravity}'s higher-spin gravity complexity chapter
 \textup{(}\S\ref*{ch:thqg-gravitational-complexity}%
 \textup{)} mentions the $E_\infty$-topologization tower;
 the present functor lands in $E_3^{\mathrm{top}}$. The
 $E_\infty$-topologization tower is handled by
 Vol~I~\ref*{V1-thm:topologization-tower} and is
 outside the scope of this chapter.
\end{enumerate}

Each restriction is structural; none falsifies the theorem.
