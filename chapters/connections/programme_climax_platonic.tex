\section{The master theorem of this volume}
\label{sec:climax-master-theorem}

The master theorem of Volume~II is the \(\Xi\)-relative construction
and naturality of the \emph{Universal Holography functor}
\[
 \Phihol\colon \ChirAlgclimax^{\omega, \mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_{X}
 \;\longrightarrow\;
 \HTQFTclimax^{\Xi}_{X \times \mathbb{R}},
\]
\[
 (A,\omega,\mathrm{BL},\Xi) \;\longmapsto\;
 \Bigl(
 \eta^\partial_A\colon\mathrm{boundary}\xrightarrow{\simeq} A,\;\;
 \chi_{\mathrm{HT},A}\colon
 Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)\xrightarrow{\simeq}\mathrm{bulk},\;\;
 \mathrm{interaction} = \SCchtop\text{-action via braces}
 \Bigr).
\]
Here \(\ChirAlgclimax^{\omega,\mathrm{BL,adm},\Xi}_X\) denotes the
admissible boundary-linear source subcategory of
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-functor}: chirally Koszul
chiral algebras on a
smooth projective curve $X$ equipped with a conformal vector $\omega$
at non-critical level, a choice of boundary line $\mathrm{BL}$, and
the exact \(\Xi\)-datum needed for the chosen HT realisation and
bulk--Hochschild comparison.
The target category collects three-dimensional holomorphic-topological
gauge theories on $X \times \mathbb{R}$ equipped with their
\(\Xi\)-comparison maps. The triple
$(\mathrm{boundary},\mathrm{bulk},\mathrm{interaction})$ is a
$\SCchtop$-pair: bulk $Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)$ acts on
boundary $A$ via the chiral brace operation, and topologisation by
the inner conformal vector $\omega$ at non-critical level promotes
$\SCchtop$ to $E_{3}$-topological.

The \emph{first non-trivial instance} is the
\emph{holographic boundary-CFT description of pure $3d$
gravity} in the sense of Brown--Henneaux 1986
\cite{BrownHenneaux} and Witten 2007~\cite{Witten2007},
obtained by setting $A = \Vir_{c}$ at $c \neq 0$ and
non-critical: the bulk factorisation algebra is the chiral
derived centre of the Virasoro vacuum module, the boundary is
Virasoro itself at central charge $c = 3\ell/(2G_{N})$
identified with the AdS$_{3}$ curvature--Newton ratio through
the Brown--Henneaux dictionary, and the $E_{3}$-topological
structure is the gauge-invariance of the
$3d$ holomorphic-topological bulk of pure gravity.
\emph{Scope qualifier.} The theorem does \emph{not} construct
a dynamical-metric path integral for $3d$ gravity; it
identifies the boundary Virasoro chiral algebra, its derived
chiral centre as the bulk factorisation algebra, and the
$E_{3}$-topological brace action intertwining them. The
identification with ``pure $3d$ quantum gravity'' is the
boundary-CFT / holographic reading of Witten 2007: given
Brown--Henneaux $c = 3\ell/(2G_{N})$, the Virasoro data supplies
the boundary algebraic input for the partition function, the BTZ
spectrum, and the modular sum. The physical BTZ/Cardy reading
requires the corresponding modular-invariance, vacuum-dominance, and
saddle hypotheses; the bulk is read off the boundary algebra, not
produced as an independent metric path integral. Literal
``quantum gravity'' in the dynamical-metric sense is
\ClaimStatusConjectured at this scope; see the scope remark
	at~\cref{rem:climax-qg-scope}. The \emph{operadic horizon} is
	$E_{\infty}$-topological only under the endpoint package
	\(\hypProchazka,\hypCKL,\hypPRSh,\hypYamada\): iterated Sugawara on
	$W_{\infty}[\lambda]$ supplies one further topological $E_{1}$ factor
	on $Q$-cohomology for each higher-spin Casimir current \(T^{(n)}\),
	and Dunn additivity commutes the tower in the inverse-limit ambient of
	\cref{thm:climax-w-infty-endpoint}.

\section{The question we set ourselves}
\label{sec:climax-question}

Fix a smooth projective curve $X$ over $\mathbb{C}$ and a chirally
Koszul chiral algebra $A$ on $X$ carrying a conformal vector
$\omega$ at non-critical level. Two structures present themselves.

The first is algebraic: the derived chiral centre
\[
 Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A) \;=\; \mathrm{ChirHoch}^{\bullet}(A, A),
\]
a bar-computable cochain complex equipped with a brace operation
over the chiral operad $\mathrm{FM}_{k}(X)$. It is, by
Theorem~A$^{\infty,2}$ (\cref{V1-thm:A-infinity-2}, Vol~I), the
natural receptacle of chiral Deligne on $A$.

The second is physical only after a realization datum has been chosen:
\(\Xi(A)\) supplies a three-dimensional holomorphic-topological
gauge theory \(T_{A,\Xi}\) on \(X \times \mathbb{R}\), a boundary
comparison
\(\eta^\partial_A\colon\mathrm{Obs}^{\partial}(T_{A,\Xi})\simeq A\),
and a bulk factorisation algebra
\(\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{bulk}}(T_{A,\Xi})\) on the half-space
geometry.

Universal holography asserts the corresponding sequent. The concrete
form of this assertion is the comparison map
\[
 \chi_{\mathrm{HT},A}\colon
 Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)
 \xrightarrow{\simeq}
 \mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{bulk}}(T_{A,\Xi})
\]
at the level of factorisation algebras carrying the correct
$E_{3}$-topological structure on $X \times \mathbb{R}$. The
question is where this equivalence holds at chain level: on the
original complex, on a strict completed/pro surface, or only after a
coderived localization, as $A$ ranges through the standard landscape.

\begin{remark}[Sequent form of the maximal theorem]
\label{rem:universal-holography-sequent-form}
Every line of the master theorem is read as the sequent
\[
\Xi(A)\ \vdash_{\mathcal A(A)}
\left(
\Phihol(A,\Xi_A)=T_{A,\Xi},\quad
\mathrm{Obs}^{\partial}(T_{A,\Xi})\simeq A,\quad
\mathrm{Obs}^{\mathrm{bulk}}(T_{A,\Xi})\simeq
Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(A)
\right),
\]
with status
\[
\sigma(A)\in
\{\ClaimStatusProvedHere,\ \ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere,
\ClaimStatusConditional,\ \ClaimStatusConjectured\}.
\]
Here \(\Xi(A)\) is the named hypothesis package and
\[
\mathcal A(A)=
\begin{cases}
\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Vect}) &
 A\in\{\mathbf G,\mathbf L,\mathbf C\}\ \text{on the stated
 non-critical loci},\\
\widehat{\mathrm{Ch}}_{\mathrm{wt},\rho}\ \text{or}
 \mathrm{pro}\text{-}\mathrm{Ch}(\mathrm{Vect}) &
 A\in\mathbf M,\\
\varprojlim_N\mathcal A_{\le N}^{\mathrm{wt}} &
 A=W_{\infty}[\lambda].
\end{cases}
\]
The raw direct-sum statement is not a theorem in class~\(\mathbf M\).
For arbitrary logarithmic \(\SCchtop\)-algebras the algebraic package
exists, but the physical observable identification requires \textup{(H1)},
\textup{(H2)}, and the exact-sector comparison.  At
\(k=-h^{\vee}\), the affine object is outside \(\Phihol\)'s
non-critical source.  For \(W_{\infty}[\lambda]\) the displayed
bounded-weight pro-window tower is the object; a uniform Banach algebra
is not asserted.  For the K3/Borcherds lane the scalar shadow is proved
only at scalar level unless the Hall--Borcherds operator gates are
constructed.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[\label{rem:climax-master-vs-pointwise}
Functorial theorem versus pointwise theorem]
\cref{thm:universal-holography-master} is the \emph{functorial}
master statement; \cref{thm:programme-climax} below is its
\emph{pointwise} content with the explicit Banach-completion radius
$\rho_{*}(A) = |c|/\beta_{A}$ and the explicit identification of
the bulk factorisation algebra. The pointwise version is what the

inherited from
Theorem~\ref{thm:universal-holography-functor}
(\texttt{universal\_holography\_functor.tex}). Both are proved at
the same conditional scope.
\end{remark}

\begin{equation}\label{eq:phihol-factorization}
\Phihol \;=\; \Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}_{\mathrm{bic}}
 \;\circ\; \mathsf{Prim}^{\mathrm{bic}},
\end{equation}

\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
\emph{Bicoloured primitive presentation.}
Apply Vol~II
\cref{thm:bicoloured-primitive-universality}: every
$\SCchtop$-algebra $(A, \omega, \mathrm{BL})$ on $X \times \R$ is
canonically presented by its bicoloured primitive package
$\primPkgBicolour$, with $A = A_b = \mathrm{End}_{\openFactCat}(b)$
the chart algebra at boundary vacuum $b \in \openFactCat$.

\emph{Universal stage-chain action.}
The universal stage chain action of Vol~II
\cref{cor:bicoloured-primitive-universality-3dHT} sends
$\primPkgBicolour$ to a coherent stratification
$(\bdyAlg, \BarTwc{\bdyAlg}, \Zderch(\bdyAlg), \mathrm{Line}(\bdyAlg))$
across the levels $\mathsf{C} \to \mathsf{S} \to \mathsf{Z} \to \mathsf{A}$.
The level-$\mathsf{Z}$ object $\Zderch(\bdyAlg) \simeq
\bulkChirHoch{\bdyAlg}$ is precisely the bulk identified by
clause~(M3) of \cref{thm:universal-holography-master}; the
boundary recovery in (M4) is the level-$\mathsf{C}$ chart algebra.

\emph{Brace naturality.}
The brace $\SCchtop$-naturality of (M5) lifts
through the bicoloured nine-tuple's coherence relations
\textup(directional restriction $\SCchtop(\ldots, \mathsf{top}, \ldots; \mathsf{cl})
= \varnothing$ + Voronov's Swiss-cheese axioms\textup): the
half-braiding $\HalfBraid$ of the nine-tuple mediates the
bulk-to-boundary OPE residue, which is the action of bulk on
boundary in (M5). Functoriality of arrows in (M6) is the
arrow-component of the equivalence
$\mathsf{Prim}^{\mathrm{bic}}$.

\emph{HT realisation.}
The functor
$\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}_{\mathrm{bic}}$ is well-defined on the
target of $\mathsf{Prim}^{\mathrm{bic}}$ by the universal stage
chain action; the composite $\Phi^{\mathrm{HT}}_{\mathrm{bic}}
\circ \mathsf{Prim}^{\mathrm{bic}}$ recovers $\Phihol$ on the
master theorem's scope by the universal stage chain action's
naturality on $\primPkgBicolour$ \textup(Vol~II
\cref{cor:bicoloured-primitive-universality-3dHT}\textup).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Architectural reading of the factorisation]
\label{rem:phihol-factorization-architectural}

\emph{structural reason} for the Universal Holography master

half-braiding $\HalfBraid$ of the bicoloured nine-tuple. Universal
Holography is the universal stage chain made physical.
\end{remark}

\begin{theorem}[Borcherds-row separation on the orthogonal-Shimura CHL

 =

 =
0,
\end{equation}

 \in

 \qquad

\end{equation}

\item The finite table in
\cref{rem:climax-kappa-tuple-K3xE-counterexample} is the whole

\end{enumerate}

\begin{proof}
The Vol~I census defines the Hodge row used in the \(\kappa\)-tuple as
the Hodge supertrace
\(\sum_q(-1)^q h^{0,q}(X)\).  For the K3-fibered Calabi--Yau

\[
(h^{0,0},h^{0,1},h^{0,2},h^{0,3})=(1,1,1,1),
\qquad
1-1+1-1=0.
\]

\cite[Theorem 13.3]{Borcherds95} and the

admissible levels give the five values displayed in

\((\kappaChHodge,\chi(\cO_{K3}))=(0,2)\), or of
\((\kappaChHodge,\kappaFiber)=(0,24)\), is constant on the window.
The Borcherds row is not constant.  This proves the asserted row
separation and the failure of the additive identity.
\end{proof}

($C_{\text{int}} = 12$)?
The $W$-line integer $a_n$ upgrades to an \emph{algebraic
closed form} (square-root generating function) in contrast to the
$T$-line's \emph{transcendental closed form}
(polynomial plus $\log$), reflecting the \emph{linear-vs-quadratic
recurrence dichotomy}
(Vol~I
Remark~\ref*{V1-rem:linear-vs-quadratic-asymptotic-recurrence}): $T$-line's
leading-$c$ cascade collapses the quadratic shadow recursion to a
linear recurrence on $A_r$, whereas the $W$-line's integer
normalisation preserves the quadratic structure, giving
Catalan-type sub-exponential growth $\sim n^{-5/2}$.
\end{remark}

\begin{proof}
The architecture has three pillars: a convergence pillar, a
rigidity pillar, and a gauge-theoretic pillar. Each is a consistency
constraint verified independently on the supplied datum \(\Xi(A)\);
none constructs the comparison map \(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},A}\), whose
existence is part of the hypothesis \(\Xi(A)\). The theorem asserts
properties (i)--(iii) of the supplied data, and these are the
intersection of the three pillars.

\emph{Convergence pillar (tempered-stratum heal).} For the shadow-
tower coefficients $S_{r}(A)$ of any $A$ in the standard landscape,
\[
 \limsup_{r \to \infty} \Bigl(\frac{|S_{r}(A)|}{r!}\Bigr)^{1/r}
 \;=\; 0.
\]
The proof is Stirling dominance:
$(r!)^{1/r} \sim r/e$ grows without bound; any finite Arnold
coupling $\beta_{A}$ gives
$|S_{r}(A)|^{1/r} \le \beta_{A}/|c|$, hence the quotient tends to
zero. The corresponding ordinary generating series
$\sum_{r} S_{r}(A) \rho^{r}$ converges absolutely for
$\rho < \rho_{*}(A) = |c|/\beta_{A}$.

The Arnold couplings are explicit:
$\beta_{\mathrm{Vir}} = 6$ by direct evaluation of the Laurent
ratio $A_{r+1}/A_{r} = -6r/(r+1)$
(\cref{V1-thm:shadow-tower-asymptotic-closed-form});
$\beta_{W_{N}} = 12(H_{N} - 1) = \sum_{s=2}^{N} 12/s$
conditional on the harmonic beta law in the per-spin decomposition
(\cref{conj:beta-N-harmonic-closed-form};
\cref{thm:wn-tempered-all-N} for the $\beta$-independent Stirling
dominance on the finite-envelope loci);
$\beta_{\mathrm{Com}} = 6 \cdot |c_{K}(k)|$ by parafermion
reduction to the Virasoro sub-channel in the Kac-regular locus
(\cref{thm:irrational-coset-tempered}). In each proved or explicitly
assumed finite-envelope case $\beta_{A}$ is finite, and Stirling
dominance closes the convergence pillar.

\emph{Rigidity pillar (finite amplitude data, not Zhu alone).}
The Zhu algebra records a finite algebraic shadow on rational/lisse
rows, but finite-dimensionality of $\mathrm{Zhu}(A)$ does not bound
the cumulative Massey tower in bar degree.  The proved tempering
rows close by their own arity-growth controls: class~$\mathsf G$ and
class~$\mathsf L$ finite-depth transfer, the Virasoro recurrence,
the finite-envelope hypotheses for principal $W_N$, and finite
orbifold BV descent for the Schellekens and Monster rows.  For the
logarithmic triplet $\mathcal{W}(p)$, the Zhu-bounded-Massey route
fails: Gurarie 1993 and Flohr 1996 construct logarithmic-CFT
amplitudes whose Massey products accumulate super-polynomially
across bar degrees despite finite-dimensional Zhu algebra.  The
tempering of $\mathcal{W}(p)$ is therefore open at the current scope
(see
Remark~\ref{rem:wp-tempering-scope} in the companion chapter
\texttt{logarithmic\_wp\_tempered\_analysis\_platonic.tex}).

The refinement is necessary. $C_{2}$-cofiniteness by itself is not
the operative proof datum; the witness
\cref{prop:zhu-unbounded-tempered-nontrivial}
produces the parafermion $K(\mathfrak{sl}_{2}, \sqrt{2})$ with
infinite Zhu algebra yet tempered shadow growth. The correct
criterion on non-logarithmic algebras is Virasoro-sub-channel-in-
Kac-regular-locus plus bounded generator-to-generator OPE pole
order (\cref{cor:tempered-criterion-refined}).

\emph{Gauge-theoretic pillar (Costello-Gwilliam factorisation with
Dijkgraaf-Witten descent data).} The boundary algebra $A$ determines
a three-dimensional holomorphic-topological theory via the
factorisation envelope construction of Costello-Li
\cite{CL16} extended by the Costello-Gwilliam orbifold BV
mechanism \cite{CG21}. For class-M lattice-VOA orbifolds, the
Dijkgraaf-Witten anomaly
\[
 \alpha_{\mathrm{orb}} \;\in\; H^{3}(B\mathbb{Z}/n; U(1))
\]
is a finite-group obstruction class. Kapustin-Saulina-type local
formulae compare it with determinant and cocycle data such as
$\alpha = \mathrm{sign}(\det(1 - \sigma \vert_{\Lambda})) \cdot
\epsilon \vert_{\Lambda^{\sigma}}$; they are not a universal
vanishing theorem. The Monster case is the Leech $\mathbb Z/2$ row
with the normalized local BV sign and finite-orbifold descent supplied
(\cref{prop:monster-alpha-explicit-zero}), and the seventy-one
Schellekens $c = 24$ rows are governed by the three-stratum criterion
\[
 71 = 24_{\mathrm{Niemeier}} + 1_{V_1=0} + 46_{\mathrm{nonlat}}
\]
whose middle row is represented by the Leech-$\mathbb{Z}/2$
moonshine orbifold; the remaining non-lattice rows are Leech
$\mathbb{Z}/n$ deep-hole orbifolds
(\cref{thm:schellekens-71-all-alpha-zero}) and require their local
DW/BV data.

\emph{Intersection.} Convergence gives the Banach completion
$\mathcal{B}_{\rho}(A)$ for $\rho < \rho_{*}(A)$.
Rigidity gives the bounded Massey structure that makes the
$E_{3}$-brace on $\mathcal{B}_{\rho}(A)$ chain-level exact (not
merely up to coherent homotopy). The gauge-theoretic pillar
identifies $\mathcal{B}_{\rho}(A)$ as the bulk factorisation
algebra of the three-dimensional theory constructed by
Costello-Li on the locus where the required anomaly trivialization and
BV descent data are present. Their intersection is the chain-
level comparison \(\chi_{\mathrm{HT},A}\) asserted in clause~(ii).
\end{proof}

\section{What universal holography does not say}
\label{sec:climax-limits}

\cref{thm:programme-climax} asserts the equivalence \emph{on the
standard landscape at generic parameter}. Three scope boundaries
deserve explicit acknowledgment.

First, the parameter locus is generic in the sense of the severe
Kac-discriminant complement. At measure-zero Kac collisions the
Banach completion degenerates; the chain-level equivalence holds
in the limit but not at the singular point itself.

Second, the critical level $k = -h^{\vee}$ for affine Kac-Moody is
excluded: Sugawara degenerates, the conformal vector ceases to be
inner, and the $E_{3}$ structure collapses to an $E_{2}$ structure
realising the Feigin-Frenkel centre. This is the single
exceptional stratum in the standard landscape; it carries its own
geometry (opers on the formal disk) rather than the
$E_{3}$-topological structure.

Third, the tempered criterion VSKR + BGG is strictly weaker than
$C_{2}$-cofiniteness. The non-tempered stratum, were it to exist,
would contain chiral algebras violating VSKR or exhibiting
unbounded generator-to-generator OPE pole order. No such algebra
has been constructed in the standard landscape; the conjectural
existence of one is an open problem whose resolution would require
either an explicit construction or a proof of universal
tempering.

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

half-space lives at level $\mathsf S$ as the protected Borcherds
shadow per
\cref{thm:kappa-tuple-primitivity-orthogonal-shimura}, whose

$\beta$ (Hall--Borcherds intertwiner) and $\gamma$

\emph{CP2 source level.} $\Phitenun = \Deltafive^2$ at level

\cref{rem:three-lanes-face-6}\textup). The CP2 target is the

\emph{CP3 source level.} The classical $\lambda$-Jacobi PVA at
level $\mathsf Z$ is the cohomological bulk of the classical 3D HT
Poisson sigma model \textup(Khan--Zeng \cite{KZ25}\textup). The CP3
target is the all-loop quantum HT theory at level $\mathsf Z$,
identified via $E_3$-lift on $Q_{\mathrm{tot}}$-cohomology. The

\emph{CP4 source level.} For chiral algebras outside the Vol~I
positive-energy class
\textup(\cref{rem:chiral-positselski-residual} four obstruction
loci\textup), the bar coalgebra $\BarTwc{A}$ at level $\mathsf S$ is

\emph{Coverage.} The four CPs occupy four distinct
level-promotions: CP1 at $\mathsf S \to \mathsf Z$ (trace lane),
CP2 at $\mathsf S \to \mathsf A$ (open lane via Pentagon trace),
CP3 at $\mathsf Z \to \mathsf Z$ within the open lane (classical to
quantum), CP4 at $\mathsf S \to \mathsf Z$ (open lane outside
positive-energy class). Their union is the full set of open
level-promotions in the architectural cut.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The level stratification and the proved core]
\label{rem:cp-stratification-and-proved-core}
The proved core occupies the levels \emph{between} the Construction
Problems' source and target. CP1's
source $\Deltafive$ is the proved
Vol~III $\kappaBKM$-row witness (Gritsenko 1999); its target
$\operatorPrim{X}$ is the open obligation. CP2's source
$\Phitenun = \Deltafive^2$ is the proved
$\Phihol|_{A = \mathrm{Vir}_c}$ Pentagon-trace on the K3-Heisenberg
witness $\{5, 5, 5\}$
\textup{(\cref{rem:three-lanes-face-6}\textup)};
its target gravity-line operator is the open obligation. CP3's
source classical $\lambda$-Jacobi is the proved Vol~II PVA descent
\textup{(}\cref{thm:cohomology-PVA-main}, classical clauses (i),
(ii), unconditional\textup{)}; its target all-loop quantum HT is
conditional on the four hypotheses. CP4's source is the proved
chiral Positselski cross-volume synthesis
\textup{(\cref{thm:chiral-positselski-cross-volume}\textup)} on the
Vol~I positive-energy class; its target is the refined-ambient
extension off the class. Closing each CP \emph{collapses} one face
of the universal stage chain into a chain-level identity at the
target level and updates the proved-core table.
\end{remark}
