\chapter*{This repository within the programme}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{This repository within the programme}

This monograph is the integrated treatment shared by the mathematics
repositories: one canonical spine, byte-identical wherever it is carried,
together with the audit and obstruction chapters merged in from the
ordered-chiral reconstruction.

The programme has three volumes. \textbf{Volume I} is ordered chiral
geometry, \textbf{Volume II} is the mixed holomorphic--topological theory
of this repository, and \textbf{Volume III} is Calabi--Yau quantum groups.
The universal chiral BV and Einstein completion accompanies them without
yet having a repository of its own. Igusa--Borcherds theory is not a
volume of this sequence: it is the subject of the standalone monograph
\emph{The Igusa Square Root}.

The home volume of this repository is \textbf{Volume II}: Hamiltonian
jets, shifted cotangent fields, universal branes, Duflo quantization, and
the all-arity open--closed map. It is placed first; the remaining material
follows.

The spine files carry upstream names running from \texttt{Volume\_I} to
\texttt{Volume\_V}, in which Calabi--Yau quantum groups is labelled
\texttt{IV}. Those labels are upstream identifiers and are not the
programme numbering. They are left unchanged so that the files remain
byte-identical across repositories.
